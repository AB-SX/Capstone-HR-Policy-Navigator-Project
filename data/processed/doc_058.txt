Relocating to France
France requires contracts to be individually drafted by the Legal team.
Team member relocating to France will also receive an invite to Mihi (time tracking)
If a team member relocating to France is an expatriate who has not been a French tax
resident for the 5 calendar years before the calendar year during which they relocate,
and they wish to assess their eligibility for the impatriate tax regime, they must let
People Operations know three months in advance that they will be undertaking the
assessment. The People Operations team can reach out to legal-
employment@gitlab.com for support with appropriate contract language once the
relocating team member confirms their eligibility. Note that eligibility is a complex tax
matter and the relocating team member should check with their personal tax advisor.
Relocating from France
The relocating team member needs to resign from GitLab France S.A.S. with a notice
date effective one day before the start date of their new contract. Please email the
resignation letter to legal-employment@gitlab.com and also upload the resignation
letter to your Workday profile. This is required to process your exit in France while you
are simultaneously onboarded in the new country.
Once the resignation letter has been emailed to legal-employment@gitlab.com and
uploaded to Workday, the People Operations team member will share this letter with
the team member relocating away from France for e-signature via DocuSign as soon as
possible (and at least within 7 days) in order to waive the balance of the notice period
