Corporate Bonus Plan
The new corporate bonus program is a cash bonus opportunity for eligible GitLab team members
who are not already on the Director+ bonus plan or a sales/incentive plan.
The target bonus is 10% of base salary. This is a target bonus, which means payout is not
automatically 10% of base salary.
This program is effective June 1, 2026. Starting in 2027, it will follow our standard fiscal year cycle,
running February 1 – January 31.
What This Means
Eligible team members will have a target bonus opportunity equal to 10% of base salary, paid out
twice per year - half for each six-month period of the bonus cycle. This keeps recognition tied to
performance throughout the year, while staying aligned with our broader performance processes
and ACR (annual compensation review) timeline. While the target is 10% of base salary, payout is
commensurate with performance rating and budget and may pay between 80% - 120% of target.
