FY’ 22 Q4 Pulse Engagement Survey Results
Our Pulse Engagement Survey FY'22 Q4 ran from 2021-11-4 to 2021-11-18 and was open to all
team members who started prior to 2021-10-15. We reached 87% participation and an overall
engagement score of 85% favorable.
This score is 9 pts higher than our New Tech peer group, which consists of ~150 fast growing and
disruptive tech companies who have ~1000+ total team members.
We also scored 4 pts higher than we did in the 2020 Engagement Survey overall.
Below you will find a summary of our overall results, as well as key insights and opportunities.
A huge thanks to everyone who completed the survey and made their voice heard. Your feedback
helps us to continually improve the experience of working at GitLab.
