Purpose
This policy specifies requirements related to the use of GitLab computing resources and data
assets by GitLab Team Members so as to protect our customers, Team Members, contractors,
company, and other partners from harm caused by both deliberate and inadvertent misuse. Our
intention in publishing this policy is to outline information security guidelines intended to protect
GitLab assets.
It is the responsibility of every member of our Community to interact with GitLab computing
resources and data in a secure manner and to that end we provide the following acceptable use
standards related to computing resources, company and customer data, mobile and tablet
devices, and removable and external media storage devices.
Scope
This policy applies to all GitLab Team Members, contractors, advisors, and contracted parties
interacting with GitLab computing resources and accessing company or customer data.

Procedure
Acceptable Use and Security Requirements of Computing Resources at GitLab
GitLab-managed assets are provided to conduct GitLab business, with consideration given for
limited personal use subject to any conflicting statements contained in any individual
employment contracts or agreements. Our company uses global electronic communications and
resources as routine parts of our business activities. Personal and professional use of GitLab-
managed assets are subject to security monitoring and protection, unless prohibited under the
local laws of a respective Team Member’s jurisdiction. It is essential that electronic resources used
to perform company business are protected to ensure that these resources are accessible for
business purposes and operated in a cost-effective manner, that our company’s reputation is
protected, and that we minimize the potential for legal risk.
Those receiving GitLab-provided assets are responsible for exercising good judgment when using
GitLab-managed computers and accessing GitLab-managed data.
As per the onboarding issue procedures outlined in our handbook, evidence of device encryption
and device serial number must be provided to IT Ops prior to the completion of onboarding
period.
We are currently using Fleet as our endpoint management solution for Mac laptops. All macOS
laptops procured by GitLab will come configured with Fleet. GitLab Team Members procuring and
expensing Mac Laptops will require their Mac to be enrolled in Fleet as part of their Day 1 Security
tasks. Upon their discretion, CorpSec may install additional security tools via Fleet.
We are currently using Fleet as our endpoint management solution for Linux laptops. GitLab
Team Members using a Linux laptop must enroll their device in Fleet as part of their Day 1
Security tasks. Once a device is enrolled, Fleet automatically installs and continuously enforces
the required security tooling, including Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR). See the Linux
Desktop OS page for the full list of Linux endpoint requirements. Upon their discretion, CorpSec
may install additional security tools via Fleet.
GitLab issued assets are company property and must be treated with care and respect. If
applying stickers to company assets:
Ensure all content is professional and appropriate for a workplace environment
Do not apply stickers that could damage the device or interfere with its operation
Do not cover ventilation ports, fans, or other functional elements
Do not apply anything to the screen or areas that could obstruct the screen when closed
Security and Proprietary Information
All GitLab data is categorized and must be handled in accordance with the Data Classification
Standard. All computing assets that connect to any part of the GitLab network, or 3rd party
services that are used by GitLab, must comply with the applicable standards.

Customer Support Data Exception
While access to RED data on non-approved systems is generally prohibited as outlined in the
GitLab Data Classification Standard, a limited exception exists for team members providing
technical support to customers.
Team members are permitted to migrate RED classified customer data to their GitLab-managed
laptops solely for the purposes of providing customer support services. This exception is subject
to the following requirements:
Data migration is only permitted for active customer support cases
Customer data must be promptly deleted immediately upon resolution of the support
case
On macOS, this must include emptying the Trash, or using the rm command-line utility
(which skips the Trash).
All data handling activities must be logged and documented within the ZenDesk ticket
Important: Migrating customer data to local machines is prohibited for all other business
operations outside of providing customer support. Team members requiring similar data access
for other purposes must follow the Security and Technology Policy Exception Process or contact
the Security or Privacy teams with questions before proceeding.
Open source libraries
Open Source libraries are allowed under this policy if its use complies with the pre-approved or
approval requirements captured in the Product Legal Handbook.
Freeware, Browser Extensions, Add-ons, and Plugins
Individual Use Software (freeware, add-ons and plugins) is permitted, with the exception of
unauthorized Google Workspace Integrations and Chrome Extensions. Individual Use Software is
subject to retroactive removal at any time by IT, Legal and Security when it is deemed unsafe or
insecure.
Authorized Google Workspace Integrations and other approved Individual Use Software can be
found on the Pre-Approved Individual-Use Software list and are permitted for use subject to any
“Notes and Restrictions” for each respective Pre-Approved software offering.
To request approval for new Individual Use Software, please follow the Individual Use Software
request process.
Note
In order to abide by the SAFE Framework, to preempt third party processing of company data
under permissive individual use licensing terms, or to prevent Team Member misuse of certain
Third Party Software features, IT, Legal, Privacy and Security will generally err on the side of
disallowing exceptions to enterprise level software, particularly if there is an enterprise option
already in our Tech Stack Applications that achieves a similar purpose.
Unacceptable Use
Team Members and contractors may not use GitLab-managed resources for activities that are
illegal or prohibited under applicable law, no matter the circumstances.
