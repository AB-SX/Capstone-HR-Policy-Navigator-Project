037. People Group Program Management
People Group Program Management
This page provides an overview of Program Management within the People Group today.
Overview
Program Managers in the People Group drive alignment, measurement, and execution of our
most impactful business objectives and deliverables. They keep our division organized, on track
and connected through effective project management while maintaining agility and cross-
functional collaboration.
Key Competencies
Regardless of the team, our Program Management key competencies are consistent to ensure
the function at its core remains aligned. While the job families remain the SSOT for roles across
GitLab, a few key competencies and behaviors to expect from all Program Managers within the
People Group are:
Key Competency Behavior
Subject matter expertise Guides programs within their functional area
Effective communication Provide regular project status updates and project visibility to stakeholders
Growth mindset Drive continuous improvement through retrospectives and optimization
Change management Facilitate pivots and timeline adjustments effectively
Stakeholder management Keep teams aligned and accountable for executing deliverables
Strategic planning Ensures work aligns with the division’s and organization’s overall goals
Program methodologies Document processes, manage project plans and mitigate risks
Note: Company-wide competencies can be referenced here and company-wide job frameworks can be
referenced here. The competencies outlined above are intended to complement these existing artifacts,
not replace them.
Collaboration Framework
When multiple Program Managers work on the same project:
The Program Manager from the DRI team leads end-to-end management
Other Program Managers partner from their team’s prospective
Close Collaboration ensures unified execution

038. People Operations Team
People Operations Team
GitLab People Operations Department Handbook
Welcome to the People Operations Team Handbook
Our Mission is to provide extraordinary People support by developing and implementing
policies, programs, and services that contribute to our team members’ healthy, safe, and
productive work environment.
People Operations Teams at GitLab
Here are how the team members divide their work to ensure the best results:
The People Operations Team is responsible for onboarding, career mobility,
offboarding, bonus processing, People Operations, personal information oversight, new
hire/anniversary swag, and more. This team is also responsible for job changes
(promotions and transfers), relocations, basic People data reporting, and process
liaisons during new policy and program implementations.
The Employment Solutions Team is responsible for our country and location roadmap
as we consider which countries to hire in, and which employment solution is best. The
team participates in the ‘Business Expansion Committee’ to develop a plan of country
conversions, new country creations and changes in employment solutions to reduce risk
and improve the team member experience. This team also supports relocations and
visa/sponsorship inquiries and needs.
The People Compliance Team works collaboratively to partner, manage and protect our
Team Members personal and professional information, ensuring documentation
confidentiality, and implements global compliance requirements to help meet internal
stakeholder expectations.
The People Tools & Technology Team
The People Analytics Team is responsible for working with stakeholders to develop and
report various People Metrics and KPIs for the business. Additional insight is also
providing using statistical analysis or experimental design to help understand effects of
different People related initiatives.
