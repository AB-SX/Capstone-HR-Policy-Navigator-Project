NOT Key Talent
A Principal Engineer who is the only expert in a critical system but performs at
“Performing” level with no demonstrated growth potential (This may signal a ‘single
point of failure’ issue requiring succession planning, not Key Talent)
An Account Executive with deep, hard-to-replace customer relationships but performs at
“Performing” level with no interest in increased scope (retention issue, not Key Talent)
A Manager with 10+ years and institutional knowledge but performs at “Performing”
level and is content with current scope (retention strategy needed, not Key Talent)
Note: “Exceeding” performance alone doesn’t automatically qualify someone as Key Talent. They
must also demonstrate ability and desire for broader responsibilities, outsized business impact,
and significant impact on critical functional areas.
Single Point of Failure
A Single Point of Failure (SPOF) is any person whose failure or absence would cause significant
operational disruption, prevent critical work completion, or result in substantial knowledge loss
that cannot be readily covered by others.
CRITICAL DISTINCTION: Being a SPOF does NOT automatically make someone Key Talent. Key
Talent requires surpassing performance expectations AND readiness for greater scope, not only
possessing unique knowledge, relationships, or capabilities critical to operations.
Process to Determine Key Talent
Direct managers make initial Key Talent recommendations in Workday during talent
assessment (also indicating Performance and Growth Potential assessment and SPOF
status)
Leaders discuss and calibrate recommendations during divisional calibration sessions
E-Group member provides final approval at the divisional level
Organizational Value
A team member’s organizational value should not be determined solely by Key Talent
designation. While Key Talent represents ~10% of our workforce, approximately 60-65% are core
performers (“Performing”) who keep things consistently moving forward. This group is essential
to organizational success.
Impact of Being Identified as Key Talent
We use several engagement tools to retain Key Talent:
Learning & Development: Support Key Talent growth by providing tools and
opportunities for personal and professional development.
Growth Opportunities: Discuss motivation and ambitions with Key Talent.
Opportunities include expanding scope within current role, lateral moves, or
promotions.

Individual Growth Plans (IGPs)
Key Talent Engagement Discussions can be a great starting point to start thinking through

042. Talent brand
Talent brand
Our ambitions as an employer, the steps we take to achieve and communicate those ambitions,
and the measures in place to ensure we live up to them.
Introduction
At GitLab, our mission is simple: to enable everyone to contribute to and co-create the software
that powers our world. Our platform doesn’t just bring people together—it unites teams and
organizations, breaking down barriers and redefining what’s possible in software development.
As GitLab grows, our Talent Brand team ensures our external reputation as an employer aligns
with our internal culture giving candidates a transparent view into what it’s like to be a part of our
team.
We partner across the organization to define our employer value proposition, build strong talent
pipelines, and support Talent Acquisition in attracting values-aligned candidates.
Through strategic branding and storytelling, we position GitLab as the place to grow, innovate,
and co-create the future.
Defining our talent brand vision
At GitLab, we empower people to co-create the future; of software, innovation, and their own
careers. Our platform unites teams, breaking down barriers to redefine what’s possible in
DevSecOps.
Here, you’re not just contributing to a tool, you’re shaping the future of secure, scalable software
delivery. Our high-performance culture values collaboration, ownership, and impact, accelerating
careers while driving global innovation.
This is more than a job, it’s your opportunity to build something that matters.
There are three pillars to achieving this vision:
Candidate Education to Support Conversion: Empowering candidates with clear insights
into our culture, values, and opportunities improves engagement and conversion—
accelerating time-to-hire and strengthening our employer brand.
Evolved Brand Messaging: We’re moving from a remote-first story to a bold, impact-
driven vision—Co-Create the Future—to attract talent aligned with our culture of
innovation, collaboration, and global flexibility.
Elevated Candidate Journey: We create a seamless, authentic experience that mirrors life
at GitLab—building trust, strengthening engagement, and helping candidates see
themselves in our culture from the very first touchpoint.
To achieve our vision, we need to ensure that we’re building a workplace that fits this description
by implementing these three strategic pillars in our strategy.
