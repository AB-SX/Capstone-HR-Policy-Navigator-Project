Vivvi Caregiving Benefits
Reliable backup care and tutoring services designed to support your family when you need it
most. Whether it’s unexpected childcare needs, educational support for your kids, or elder care
assistance, these benefits help reduce stress and keep your family covered.
Vivvi Caregiving Benefit Key Details
Benefit Options:
Care Cash: Reimbursement for backup care
Virtual Tutoring: Weekly 1-hour sessions for ages 5-18
Center-based and in-home backup childcare (US, Canada, UK)
In-home backup adult and elder care (US, Canada, UK)
More information on the program can be found on the Vivvi FAQ and in the Vivvi webinar
recording.

Parental Leave
Comprehensive parental leave to support you during one of life’s most important transitions.
Whether you’re welcoming a child through birth or adoption, you’ll have 16 weeks of paid leave to
bond with your new family member, adjust to your new role as a parent, and focus on what
matters most during this time.
Parental Leave Key Details
Key Features:
Available from your first day of work
Use within child’s first birthday or first anniversary of adoption
Flexible scheduling - take all at once or split into up to three segments
Runs concurrently with statutory benefits where applicable
Access to Parentaly to help with coverage planning, transitioning work, and supporting
your career through parental leave
Returning to Work:
50% capacity option available for 2-4 weeks (discuss with your manager)
Access to Parentaly support for return to work coaching
Support through #intheparenthood Slack channel
For complete details on parental leave planning, how to apply, commission policies during leave,
and resources for returning to work, please see the full Parental Leave policy handbook page.

Parental Leave Support: Parentaly
Parentaly helps you build a strong coverage plan, transition work effectively, and return to work
with confidence. You’ll gain access to templates, a personalized toolkit and materials, plus 3-9
sessions with a dedicated Parentaly Expert (before and after your leave) who has supported
hundreds of others through parental leave. Sign up instructions are outlined below.
Program Details
Open to all team members taking parental leave globally, regardless of gender or
method (adoption, surrogacy, birthing, partner giving birth)
Participants should sign up 2-3 months before starting their parental leave (though you
can also join while already on leave or early upon your return)
You’ll be matched with a Parentaly Expert and all your conversations and work will be
confidential
You’ll also receive a personalized checklist to build your coverage plan quickly and
effectively - setting you and your team up for success
Upon return, your Parentaly Expert will help you navigate whatever challenges or
priorities are top of mind - whether that is ramping back up, pursuing a promotion,
getting to know a new manager, or anything else that comes up in this transition
The program cost is funded under the Growth and Development Fund. More specific
information about the sign-up process and funding can be found in th G&D handbook
apge here
Questions about the program? Email Parentaly at info@parentaly.com

Vehicle Salary Sacrifice & Novated Leasing Programs
GitLab has evaluated vehicle salary sacrifice and novated leasing programs across multiple
regions (including Australia and the UK) and has decided not to offer these benefits at this time.
Background & Rationale
These programs typically allow team members to use pre-tax salary to cover vehicle costs
(purchase, fuel, insurance, and maintenance) through a third-party provider. In some regions,
government incentives such as Australia’s 0% FBT exemption for eligible electric vehicles or the
UK’s Electric Vehicle salary sacrifice scheme make these programs particularly attractive.
After consultation with Legal and Finance, GitLab has decided not to proceed for the following
reasons:
Liability exposure: Introducing any arrangement involving vehicles, even indirectly,
creates operational and financial exposure that is difficult to manage within GitLab’s
structure as an all-remote organization.
Financial reporting complexity: FBT reporting obligations, payroll deduction
management, and ongoing compliance requirements introduce a level of financial and
administrative complexity that does not align with GitLab’s operating model at this
stage.
