1.1 Policy Statement
1.2 Scope
The Equal Opportunity Policy (EOP) has been implemented to comply with applicable laws and
regulations which are intended to prevent discrimination against individuals with disabilities,
transgender people and protected persons under HIV/AIDS Law. This EOP applies to all persons
with disabilities, protected medical conditions and transgender people including team members,
job applicants and candidates, interns/trainees and team members who acquire any disability
during their work tenure. It covers all aspects of the employment relationship, including but not
limited to, recruitment, hiring, selection for training opportunities, compensation, transfer,
promotion, benefit eligibility, facilities access, corrective action and termination. The EOP focuses
on eliminating artificial barriers to employment or advancement and fostering a diverse
environment. Consistent with this EOP, GitLab is committed to making employment decisions
based on merit, qualifications, business needs and other job-related criteria.
GitLab will not discharge or in any other manner discriminate or retaliate against team members
or applicants because they have inquired about, discussed, or disclosed their disabilities or
medical information or details regarding their gender. This EOP is in line with the Rights of
Persons with Disabilities Act 2016, the Rights of Persons with Disability Rules 2017 (together,
Disability Law), the Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act 2019 (Transgender Act) with
Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Rules 2020 (together, Transgender Law) and the
Human Immunodeficiency Virus and Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (Prevention and
Control) Act 2017 and Human Immunodeficiency Virus and Acquired Immune Deficiency
Syndrome (Prevention And Control) Rules 2018 (together, HIV/AIDS Law).
The People Compliance team is responsible for this EOP and any necessary training on equal
opportunities. The EOP does not form part of any team member’s contract of employment and
GitLab may amend it at any time.
1.3 Appointment of Liaison and Complaints Officers
As per the applicable law, GitLab has appointed its Team Member Relations and People
Compliance Teams to act as Liaison and Complaints Officers.
The Team Member Relations and People Compliance Teams will be responsible for:
Ensuring a disability-friendly workplace - all team members are encouraged to discuss
any specific requirements that they may have with the Team Member Relations Team;
•

Taking initiatives and providing the requisite support needed to realize the goals of an
inclusive, accessible and discrimination-free workplace and of offering reasonable
accommodation;
Overseeing the recruitment of individuals protected by this policy and the provision of
facilities and amenities to them;
Increasing awareness amongst team members of GitLab’s commitment to providing
equal opportunities for all;
Developing proactive strategies to prevent discrimination or harassment against the
objectives of this EOP; and
Addressing any grievances or concerns relating to violation of this EOP.
1.4 Facilities and Amenities
The following sections outline the facilities and amenities provided by GitLab in implementing
and executing this EOP.
Digital Infrastructure
It is GitLab’s continuous endeavour to ensure that all our documents, communication and
information technology systems adhere to the accessibility standards. GitLab provides a range of 
accessibility and accommodation tools designed to support team members and ensure an
inclusive, productive work environment. If a team member requires access to an accommodation
tool, they should reach out in the #enterprise-applications Slack channel for assistance. The
Enterprise Team will coordinate with the Team Member Relations team for any accommodation-
related requests.
Reasonable Accommodation
GitLab will make reasonable accommodations for eligible/qualified team members or job
applicants with special needs and protected persons, to the extent required by applicable law to
enable them to perform the essential functions of their positions or apply for the relevant
position, as the case may be. What constitutes a reasonable accommodation may depend on
many factors including, but not limited to, the nature of the individual’s special needs and
medical requirements and the essential functions of the position. GitLab is not obligated to
provide an accommodation that would impose an undue hardship on GitLab.
Such accommodation would be provided: a) to ensure equal opportunity in the application and
selection process, b) to enable a qualified individual to perform the essential functions of their
role, and c) to enable a qualified individual to enjoy the same benefits and privileges of
employment as other employees. Examples of reasonable accommodations may include (but not
be limited to) a change or modification made to the work environment, role duties, or application
process.
If a team member requires an accommodation to be made for them, a request for
accommodation should be made by following the reasonable accommodation request process
set out in the Handbook. Candidates should follow the candidate accessibility and
accommodation request process set out in the Handbook. Preference may be given to persons
with special needs while allotting such accommodation.

Travel, Stay and Transport
While GitLab is all-remote, there may be times when team members travel for work-related
functions or co-working events. GitLab is committed to the value of a safe, violence-free work
environment and ensuring and exhibiting equal commitment to the safety and health of team
members. Team members should adhere to any recommended safety recommendations made
by the GitLab group and if support is required, team members should follow the team member
safety guidelines set out in the Handbook.
Team Member Engagement and Social Inclusion
GitLab is dedicated to providing a harassment-free and inclusive event experience for everyone
regardless of gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, neurodiversity, physical
appearance, body size, ethnicity, nationality, race, age, religion (or lack thereof), or any other
protected categories with a provision of reasonable accommodation being available to team
members with disabilities. Team members should adhere to the GitLab Events Code of Conduct
set out in the Handbook and use the GitLab Operating Principles to guide interactions.
1.5 Hiring, Recruitment and Manner of Selection
GitLab is proud to be an equal opportunity workplace and GitLab’s policies and practices relating
to recruitment and selection are based solely on merit, without regard to any legally protected
characteristics. At GitLab, all positions are open for all persons. Accommodations are available on
request and candidates should follow the candidate accessibility and accommodation request
process set out in the Handbook.
Wherever possible, vacancies will be advertised internally as well as externally. Selection criteria
are kept under constant review to ensure that they are non-discriminatory and that they relate
purely to the skills needed for the job and nothing else.
1.6 Other Facilities
Leave - GitLab may, based on a team member’s request for leave which exceeds six consecutive
days, for a reason related to their disability, administer the leave as a leave of absence through
the GitLab Sick Time policy, under which eligible team members will receive 100% paid leave for
up to 25 working days. GitLab’s Sick Time policy is administered concurrently with any statutory
sick leave for which a team member may be eligible. This will be treated as a request for
reasonable accommodation and will be evaluated accordingly. Team members should follow the 
process in the Handbook for requesting this leave of absence.
Training and Career development - GitLab does not discriminate in providing training opportunities
and any such opportunities would be available to all team members regardless of their protected
characteristics. Every effort will be made to accommodate any special needs of team members by
making reasonable adjustments to working arrangements. This applies to all aspects of
employment, including training.
