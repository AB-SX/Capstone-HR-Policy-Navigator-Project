How to add the New Hire to the Papaya platform
Log into the Papaya platform
Click the ‘add worker’ tab on the top right side of the page
Click ‘create new request’ under the EOR worker block
Select the candidates country on the drop down
Click next
You be will be on the ‘worker details’ page - fill in the candidates details
Please ignore the following: Company ID
Click next
You will now be on the ’employment terms’ page - fill in the details
Please ignore the following: expected end date in the case of an indefinite contract & the
organizational attributes
Click continue
You will be on the ‘contract compensation’ page - fill in the contract details
Please ignore the following: allowances & miscellaneous
Under contract terms, select the mandatory requirements by local law tabs for all
Click next
You will be on the ‘initial employment terms letter’ page - fill in the contract details
Should papaya send the candidate the initial employment terms letter prior to the
contract - click ‘yes’
Click next
You will now be able to review all the candidate and contract details, you are able to edit
them should you wish.
Select all the term of the consent clause
When should papaya contact the worker - select ‘as soon as possible’
Click submit
Your candidate has been submitted successfully. You will be redirected to the papaya
homepage
Once you submit the candidate, an alert will be sent to Papaya to inform them of the
new project
Amit Bengal - AmitBe@papayaglobal.com (Customer Success Manager at Papaya who
looks after GitLab) will reach out to the CES to confirm that they have received the alert.
Amit should ask for permission to contact the candidate directly - please confirm this
ASAP. Papaya will validate the information submitted into the platform and reach out to
CES or the candidate should there be any immediate questions regarding the contract
information shared
