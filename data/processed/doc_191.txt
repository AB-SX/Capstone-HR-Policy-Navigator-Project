Delivering feedback to declined candidates
At any stage a team member needs to be declined, it is up to the Recruiter and Hiring Manager to
connect to determine the best approach to ensure the appropriate feedback is given by the hiring
manager. It is important to deliver transparent and honest feedback to the candidate while
remaining constructive and focusing on growth.
If the candidate has made it to the hiring manager screen or beyond, hiring managers can
consider this approach:
Condense the themes from all of the scorecards so that there is a high level, but
anonymous summary of what they did well in the interview, and any opportunities for
improvement
Document your key takeaway as a hiring manager in terms of how this candidate does
not have the right competencies for the position
Document your key takeaways on what the candidate did well and should continue
doing, along with improving other areas to be ready in the future
Provide actionable, concrete steps to work on those areas
Sync with the candidate to review these topics in a synchronous meeting

066. Growth and Development Fund
Growth and Development Fund
A description of programs and resources for team members to access when using the G&D Fund.
The Growth and Development (G&D) Fund supports GitLab team members in pursuing approved
learning experiences that strengthen their skills, support career growth, and contribute to high
performing teams. Team members can be reimbursed for up to $10,000 per calendar year for
eligible growth experiences, provided they meet all eligibility requirements.
We’ve recently updated the G&D process, and are moving away from using issues for G&D
submissions, all requests going forward should be submitted via Compass.
Here’s a quick summary of the changes:
All new G&D requests must be submitted through Compass.
If you already have a GitLab issue open and it is in progress or already moving through
approval, you do not need to resubmit it.
The fund, budget, eligible uses, and core reimbursement rules are not changing.
Reimbursement still goes through Navan.
Compass gives team members clearer tracking and gives leaders better visibility into
how G&D funds are being used across their teams.
Easier tracking means easier to know how much money you have left!
Processing requests via Compass allows for better tracking of requests, a clearer structure for
submissions, and significantly improves the administration of the Fund.
If you already have a G&D GitLab issue open and it is already in progress or moving through
approval, there is no need to resubmit it. Existing GitLab issue-based requests can continue
through completion under the prior process.
Reach out to the Talent Development team if you require any assistance with submitting your
request.
Eligibility
To use the Growth and Development Fund, applicants must be full-time GitLab employees who
meet performance expectations in their role and are not on leave status.
