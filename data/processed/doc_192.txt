Step 2 - Submit your request in Compass
Create a new Growth & Development Fund request in Compass using the appropriate request
path for your opportunity type.
Your request should be submitted at least 30 days in advance of the beginning of your
experience when possible.
Be prepared to provide information such as:
the type of opportunity
provider or vendor details
program or event name
relevant dates
total cost
whether the request includes any travel considerations
how the opportunity supports your development goals
Step 3 - Gain approval
Once submitted, your request will route for approval based on cost tier.
Current approval tiers are:
Manager approval: $0 - $1,000
Manager + VP approval: $1,000.01 - $10,000
Managers should understand what they are approving and be able to explain the reasoning and
benefit behind the request.
Step 4 - Make your purchase and complete the opportunity
Once all required approvals have been completed, you may proceed with payment and
participation.
In most cases, individual G&D requests should be paid for by the team member who submitted
the request unless the specific opportunity type follows a different billing model.
Step 5 - Submit for reimbursement in Navan
In most cases, G&D costs are reimbursed after the opportunity has been completed.

All expenses should be claimed through Navan using the G&D expense type and following the

When submitting your reimbursement, include:

workflow
Travel expenses
Travel, lodging, and meals related to conferences or workshops must be expensed separately
from the G&D claim and should follow GitLab’s Travel and Expense policy.
These expenses are funded from the requestor’s department Travel & Expense budget, not from
the G&D allocation.
50% upfront reimbursement
All requests may use the split disbursement option when applicable:
50% of the approved opportunity cost can be reimbursed after payment
the remaining 50% can be reimbursed upon completion
This option does not apply to travel, hotel, or meal expenses.
Step 6 - Close out and share feedback
After reimbursement is complete, close out your request in the final system workflow and share
feedback if the experience would be useful for other team members.
Types of Growth and Development reimbursements
Please review the reimbursement section above before proceeding.
Conferences and live workshops
Conference registration fees are eligible for G&D reimbursement.
Travel, lodging, and meals must be expensed separately following the existing travel expense
policy.
Academic study
For academic courses relevant to your GitLab career development goals, the G&D Fund can be
used to cover tuition and enrollment.
To be eligible, the course must:
be from an accredited college or university
result in a final grade equivalent to a “B” or higher

Additional fees related to books, travel, and supplies are not covered under this category.
A final grade report or transcript is required for reimbursement.
Professional development, certifications, and courses
Certifications, licenses, and shorter non-academic courses that are relevant to your development
may be reimbursed through the G&D Fund.
A final grade report or satisfactory certificate of completion is required for reimbursement.
Self-service learning subscriptions
Providers such as Coursera, LinkedIn Learning, Reforge, and similar learning subscriptions may
be reimbursed when tied to a clear learning goal and intended course usage.
Language courses
Language learning may be eligible when the relevance to job performance is clear.
Code-based learning
GitLab will support coding-related learning opportunities regardless of department when they
support development goals.
Programming books
Books related to programming, software development, and technical skills that support career
development at GitLab can be reimbursed through the G&D Fund.
Coaching
GitLab is currently contracted with SoundingBoard Coaching and we have a direct relationship
with the vendor.
Coaching through the G&D Fund is available to team members at the Manager level and above.
If you are seeking coaching, we strongly encourage you to use SoundingBoard Coaching.
Because GitLab has a direct relationship with SoundingBoard, you will not need to put the cost on
your personal credit card. The charge can be routed directly to the appropriate cost center.
If you would prefer to use a different coaching vendor, please note that in your request so the
team can review it as part of the approval process.
Coaching requests should still follow the current eligibility requirements and any program-
specific guidance documented on this page.
Professional dues and memberships
Professional development memberships or dues relevant to your growth and development may
be reimbursed.
