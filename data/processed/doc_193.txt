Parentaly
Parentaly is an expert-led program available globally to all team members planning to go on
parental leave - including birthing and non-birthing parents. The program provides personalized
1:1 support before, during, and after leave to help you build a strong coverage plan, transition
work effectively, and return to work with confidence. You’ll gain access to templates, a
personalized toolkit and materials, plus up to nine sessions with a dedicated Parentaly Expert
who has supported hundreds of others through parental leave.
Parentaly costs $2,500 and is funded through your G&D allowance. However, unlike most G&D
opportunities, you do not pay upfront and apply for reimbursement. GitLab pays Parentaly
directly on your behalf - you simply sign up after receiving manager approval on your G&D issue.
We recommend signing up at least 2-3 months before your leave begins to get the full benefit of
the program, though sign-ups are accepted at any point in your leave journey, including when
you’re returning to work.
Reimbursement Requirements: Parentaly invoices GitLab directly. There is no payment or
reimbursement action required from you. After receiving manager approval on your G&D
request, proceed directly to sign up for the Parentaly coaching program.
Managers can also access support through Parentaly by activating a Manager Toolkit or
attending Manager Training, which provides real-time support and access to guides and
resources for leading effectively through parental leave transitions.
Wellbeing
The G&D Fund should not be used for therapy, counseling, mindfulness apps, or similar wellbeing
resources. Team members should use Modern Health and related wellbeing resources instead.
Bulk applications
If multiple team members are applying for the same opportunity, submit one request for the
group where appropriate, but each team member must still pay for and submit reimbursement
for their own expenses.
Company-directed learning opportunities
The G&D Fund supports individual career development chosen by team members. Company-
directed or mandatory training should be funded through other appropriate department
budgets.
Recoupment policy
If a team member voluntarily terminates employment before completing twelve consecutive
months of active employment, academic study or tuition expenses of $1,000 USD and over must
be refunded to GitLab.
This policy applies to academic study reimbursements only. Team members should review this
requirement before submitting requests that fall under this category.

Tax implications
In some countries, Growth & Development reimbursements may be considered taxable income.
Team members should review the country-specific tax table to understand how reimbursements
may be treated in their location.
For team members in the United States, G&D reimbursements are generally not considered
taxable income when used for qualifying work-related education expenses. However, individual
circumstances may vary.
Contact Payroll with country-specific tax questions.
FAQ
Can I use the G&D Fund to arrange training for my entire team?
No. The G&D Fund is designed for individual career development, and team-wide training should
be budgeted outside the G&D Fund.
How can I track my G&D spend?
If you want to know how much of your G&D Fund remains for the calendar year, reach out
through the #learninganddevelopment slack channel.
Does budget rollover?
No. Unspent G&D budgets do not roll over from one year to the next.
Can spare budget be shared with other team members?
No. G&D budgets are individual and cannot be shared across team members.
Can I pay for one opportunity over multiple years?
Yes. If an opportunity spans more than one year, costs can be distributed across multiple years,
but you still cannot exceed $10,000 expensed per year.
