GitLab BV (Netherlands) Benefits
GitLab BV (Netherlands) benefits specific to Netherlands based team members.
We’ve Got You Covered
Clear benefits, real support
At GitLab, we believe team member benefits should be simple to understand and there when you
need them. From life’s big moments to everyday questions, our comprehensive coverage gives
you clarity and confidence so you can focus on your work and life.
In addition to the local benefits listed below, all Netherlands team members have access to the 
company-wide benefits available to all team members globally.

healthcare costs.

Full-time employment status
Based in the Netherlands

No documentation required

CZ Collective Healthcare Policy
GitLab partners with CZ to offer discounted supplementary insurance rates through our collective
policy.
Eligibility Requirements
All GitLab Netherlands team members
Can include dependents
Coverage & Key Features
Discounted rates on supplementary insurance
Direct payment to CZ via bank account
Annual enrollment period (end of year)
Not obligatory - you can choose any approved provider
How to Enroll/Access
Review enrollment instructions in the Netherlands CZ Collective Policy document
Visit the GitLab CZ page
Contact CZ directly at 088 555 7777 for questions
Important Dates
Team members can enroll in the plans during the enrollment period at the end of each
year
Team members must cancel their old insurance before January 1 and enroll in new
insurance before February 1. The coverage will be retroactive to January 1
For more details, review the Changing Health Insurance page on the Netherlands
Government Website
Vision Reimbursement
Team members experiencing eye problems from display screen work can receive up to EUR 250
reimbursement for corrective eyewear and an eye test required in connection with screen use.
The EUR 250 benefit amount is a combined cap covering both the eye test and computer glasses
together.
Eligibility Requirements
Must have optician documentation showing lenses are specifically for display screen
work

Financial Security
Vacation Money (Vakantiegeld)
Netherlands team members receive the customary vacation money payment each May, as
defined by the government.
Key Features
Paid annually in May
Amount determined by government regulations
Statutory Sick Leave
Team members who are unable to work due to illness are entitled to 70% of their salary
for up to 104 weeks. This leave runs concurrently with GitLab’s Sick Time Policy. Team
members will receive 100% of their salary for the first 25 days of illness.
GitLab complies with Dutch local Laws regarding sick leave. Local laws override any
leave policy we have in the benefit of the team members.
Team members must select Out Sick in Workday to ensure that all sick time is properly
tracked. For complete reporting procedures, please review the sick time procedure for
The Netherlands here.
Statutory Vacation Leave
Team members are entitled to at least 20 vacation days per year which run concurrently
with GitLab PTO. The days will accrue at 1.67 days/month from the team member’s start
date. Any unused days will be carried into the next calendar year, but expire on July 1st.
Should the team member leave GitLab within the calendar year, an entitlement to a pro
rata part vacation days exists. Team members must designate all vacation days taken as 
Paid Time OFF in Workday to ensure that vacation entitlement is properly tracked.
