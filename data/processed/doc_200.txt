Retirement Plan
Starting January 1, 2021, all team members over 21 receive a private pension plan through ASR
(formerly Brand New Day) to supplement the standard state pension (AOW).
Eligibility Requirements
Must be over 21 years old
Employed via GitLab BV Netherlands contract
Automatic enrollment upon hire
Key Features
Defined Contribution Agreement with 5% flat rate of pension base
You contribute 2%, GitLab contributes 3%
Build invested pension fund for retirement income
Death benefits for partner and children during service
Incapacity coverage continues contributions if you’re unable to work
Option for voluntary additional contributions
Choice of investment profiles: neutral, defensive, offensive, or custom mix
How to Enroll/Access
Automatically enrolled through our Netherlands benefits partner
Contact ASR for questions or changes
Access training videos in the ASR resource folder
Important Details
Team members can transfer pension value from former employer within 6 months
Review the Pension Scheme document for complete details
Learn about voluntary contributions in the 2024 ANW-gap pension guide
Financial Wellbeing Support
GitLab’s Netherlands pension provider, a.s.r., partners with Geldfit, a free and anonymous
financial wellbeing service available to all Netherlands-based team members.
Geldfit can help you with:
Questions about money and your financial situation
Insights and practical tools to support your financial health
Guidance on available support options

Coverage & Key Features
2x annual salary for death or permanent disability due to accident
Maximum annual salary coverage: EUR 250,000
Flat EUR 50,000 coverage for interns
No enrollment required - automatic coverage
How to File a Claim
Complete claim form (English or Dutch)
Send to total-rewards@gitlab.com immediately
For death claims: submit at least 36 hours before funeral/cremation
Important Details
Review policy terms (English or Dutch)
Inform your spouse about this benefit
Life Insurance Through Pension
Death benefits are included in your pension scheme to protect your loved ones.
Coverage & Key Features
Lifelong partner’s pension for spouse - see section 8 of the Pension Scheme
Temporary dependent’s pension for children
The beneficiary is also entitled to an additional partner’s pension that is bought by the
invested pension capital upon death - see section 18 of the Pension Scheme

Growing Families Leaves
Statutory Maternity Leave
16 weeks of fully paid leave to welcome your new child, with flexibility in how you use it.
Leave Entitlement
Up to 6 weeks before estimated birth date
10 weeks after birth
Can reduce pre-birth leave to minimum 4 weeks and add unused days after birth
100% salary continuation (GitLab tops up UWV benefit)
The statutory leave will run concurrently with GitLab Parental Leave
How to Access
Email leaves@gitlab.com with your desired start date
Can start 6 weeks before, but no later than 4 weeks before due date
HR Savvy handles UWV application on your behalf
The benefit is always directly paid to Gitlab so that there is once source of pay”
Important Details
If unable to work from 6 weeks before due date, leave automatically begins
Runs concurrently with GitLab parental leave if eligible
Statutory Partner/Paternity Leave
Time to bond with your new child and support your partner.
Leave Entitlement
5 days paid Birth Leave (100% salary), to be used within 4 weeks of birth
5 weeks paid Additional Birth Leave within 6 months (at 70% of maximum day wage)
