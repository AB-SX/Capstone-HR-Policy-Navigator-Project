Adoption and Foster Care Leave
6 weeks of leave with adoption/foster care allowance for welcoming a child into your family.
Leave Entitlement
GitLab supplements allowance to ensure 100% pay for first 16 weeks
Additional 9 weeks partial payment available for children under 8 (effective 2022-08-02)
Must be within child’s first year after adoption/placement
How to Access
Notify leaves@gitlab.com at least 3 weeks in advance
Designate as “Parental Leave” in Workday
Parental Leave Options
Extended unpaid leave available for parents of children 8 years or younger.
Leave Entitlement
Up to 26 times your weekly work hours
Partial payment (70% of daily wage) for up to 9 weeks within child’s first year
Must be used before the child turns 8 years old
The parental leave entitlement is per child
GitLab applies for UWV benefit on your behalf
Short-Term Care Leave
Time off when family members or others close to you need essential care.
Eligibility
Must be only person available to provide care
Covers: children, foster children, spouse/partner, parents, grandparents, siblings, others
with social relationship
Leave Entitlement
Up to 2x your weekly hours per year (e.g., 80 hours for full-time)
100% pay for first 25 days (then 70% statutory rate)
How to Access
Notify manager and leaves@gitlab.com as soon as possible

Long-Term Care Leave
Extended leave for serious, life-threatening illness of child, partner, or parent.
Leave Entitlement
Up to 6x your weekly hours per year (e.g., 240 hours for full-time)
Unpaid, but runs concurrently with PTO (100% pay for first 25 days)
How to Access
Notify manager and leaves@gitlab.com as soon as possible
Time Off Policies
Sick Leave Support
Comprehensive coverage when you’re unable to work due to illness.
Leave Entitlement * 100% salary for first 25 days * 70% salary continuation for up to 104 weeks
total * GitLab complies with [Dutch local laws](https://business.gov.nl/regulation/reporting-
employee-illness-recovery/) regarding sick leave. Local laws override any leave policy we have in
the benefit of the team members How to Report * Select “Out Sick” in Workday Employees of
GitLab B.V. have further rights and responsibilities regarding sick time based on Dutch law, as
written into their employment [contracts](https://internal.gitlab.com/handbook/people-group/
people-operations/people-operations/employment_contracts/): * Team members must notify
their manager of any sick leave needed. * Team members must then enter the sick time via
Workday as aligned above. * If the sick leave extends 8 days it should be reported to the Absence
Management team via [leaves@gitlab.com](mailto:leaves@gitlab.com) by the Manager and team
member (if able). * If team members are reporting multiple instances of sick leave in a 28 day
timeframe, if this happens, it will be considered the same sickness. Please reach out to the
Absence Management team. * Once the Absence Management team has been notified of the sick
time they will inform HRSavvy. * HRSavvy will be registering the sick time in their HR portal from
the first day of absence (retrospectively). * GitLab has engaged with an occupational health and
safety centre, called Leuk Personeel, to assist employees of GitLab B.V. who may need support
while being on long-term sick leave. * If the team members is on long-term sick leave (8 days of
more), the Absence Management team will instruct HRSavvy to register the team member at the
occupational health and safety service, depending on the situation and agreement with the team
member. * Team members are expected to be available and follow the applicable Act (Gatekeeper
Act) and internal policy. ### Vacation Time Guaranteed time to rest and recharge throughout the
year. Leave Entitlement * Minimum 20 vacation days annually as per the [statutory requirements]
(https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/people-group/time-off-and-absence/time-off-types/) *
Accrue at 1.67 days/month from start date * Unused days carry over to the next calendar year but
expire July 1st * Pro-rated for partial years How to Track Designate all vacation as “Paid Time Off”
in Workday ## Questions? For any questions about your benefits, reach out to our support
