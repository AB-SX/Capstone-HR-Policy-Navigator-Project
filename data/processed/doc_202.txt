Healthcare Benefits
Healthcare Allowance
Monthly healthcare allowance of €70 providing flexibility to choose your own healthcare plan.
Healthcare Allowance Details
Eligibility
All GitLab Belgium team members
Effective from hire date
Continues during sick leave or unpaid parental leave
Key Features
€70 monthly gross allowance
Paid automatically via payroll (subject to taxation)
No documentation required
Prorated for mid-month starts/terminations

Meal Vouchers
Effective 2026-03-01, all Belgium-based team members are entitled to meal vouchers. This tax-
advantaged benefit helps cover daily meal expenses and can be used at restaurants, food stores,
and food delivery services throughout Belgium.
Eligibility Requirements
All GitLab Belgium Team Members
Prorated based on actual worked days (excludes weekends, public holidays, vacation
days, sick leave and unpaid leave)
Team members receive meal vouchers based on their worked days during the previous
month for the current month. E.g., if you worked 19 days in February, 19 meal vouchers
(nominal value: €8) will be loaded to your meal card in March
Part-time team members receive vouchers proportional to their working schedule
Team members should generally expect their Pluxee meal cards to be loaded with meal
vouchers for the previous month by the third week of each month, following payroll
submission by our local payroll provider.
Coverage & Key Features
Daily Value: €8.00 per worked day
Employer Contribution: €6.91 (tax-free)
Team Member Contribution: €1.09 (deducted from net salary)
Provider: Pluxee
Format: Electronic Meal Card
Validity: Meal vouchers are valid for 12 months from the date of issuance
