Financial Security & Retirement
Registered Retirement Savings Plan (RRSP)
Build your financial future with GitLab’s Group RRSP through Canada Life. Employer matching up
to 3% of base salary (maximum $2,500 CAD annually).
Eligibility
All full-time team members (30+ hours per week)
Effective from start date or November 1, 2021 (whichever is later)
How to Enroll
Visit mycanadalifeatwork.com
Use enrollment express PDF and video guide
Set contributions: First 3% in “Member” field, additional in “Member Voluntary”
Choose beneficiary (separate from life insurance)
Important Notes
Contribution changes allowed anytime (1-2 payroll cycles to reflect)

Time Away & Leave
Paid Time Off & Vacation
GitLab’s comprehensive PTO policy provides flexible time off while ensuring compliance with
provincial statutory requirements.
Key Features
Flexible PTO: No set annual limit - take time off as needed with manager coordination
Advance Notice: Coordinate with manager at least twice as many days in advance as
total days requested (e.g., 6 business days notice for 3 days off)
Provincial Compliance: Statutory minimums automatically tracked and managed
Usage: Designate all vacation time as “Paid Time Off” in Workday
Statutory Vacation Minimums by Province
All Provinces (except SK, QC, ON): 2 weeks after 1 year / 3 weeks after 5 years (4%
vacation pay first 5 years, 6% after)
Quebec: 2 weeks first year, 3 weeks after 3 years (4% pay first 3 years, 6% after)
Saskatchewan: 3 weeks after 1 year / 4 weeks after 10 years (6% pay first 9 years, 8%
after)
Ontario: 2 weeks after 1 year / 3 weeks after 5 years (4% pay first 5 years, 6% after)
How to Request Time Off
Discuss plans with your manager with appropriate advance notice
Coordinate with team to ensure coverage
Submit request in Workday using “Paid Time Off” category
Set up out-of-office responses and coverage plans
