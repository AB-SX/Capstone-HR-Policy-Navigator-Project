077. GitLab GK (Japan) Benefits
GitLab GK (Japan) Benefits
Discover GitLab’s benefits for team members in Japan
Clear benefits, real support
At GitLab, we believe team member benefits should be simple to understand and there when you
need them. Our Japan benefits work with the country’s statutory system to support you through
life’s biggest moments and everyday needs.
Health & Wellbeing Benefits
Medical Coverage
Overview
GitLab team members are covered through Japan’s Social Insurance system, managed by the
National Health Insurance Association (Kyokai Kenpo). This provides comprehensive medical
coverage from day one.
Eligibility
All GitLab GK (Japan) team members
Coverage effective from hire date
Key Features
Coverage includes hospital care, outpatient services, prescription medications, and specialist
treatments with standardized 30% co-payments.
How to Access Your Benefits
Receive Your Insurance Card: Provided during onboarding
Visit Healthcare Providers: Present your card at participating facilities
Pay Co-payments: 30% at time of service
Important Information
Managed by Kyokai Kenpo
For details, see the Leaflet section of the Kyokai Kenpo website
Annual Health Check-Up
Overview
You’re entitled to a comprehensive annual health check-up through your Social Insurance
benefits.
Eligibility
All team members covered by Kyokai Kenpo
Available annually

Financial Security Benefits
Employee Pension System
Overview
Automatic enrollment in Japan’s Employee Pension system provides retirement income from age
60.
Eligibility
All team members registered for Social Insurance
Requires 25+ years of contributions for full benefits
Key Features
Benefits calculated using salary history and contribution period. Contributions automatically
deducted from salary.
Unemployment Insurance (Koyou Hoken)
Overview
Government-managed unemployment insurance supports all team members with
comprehensive benefits during job transitions.
Eligibility
Working at least 20 hours per week
Expected employment of 31+ days
Includes international team members
Key Features
Covers unemployment benefits, childcare leave allowance, nursing care leave allowance, and
education/training support.
Family & Life Transition Support
Maternity Leave & Benefits
Overview
Take 14 weeks of protected maternity leave with two-thirds of your base salary through
government allowance.

Eligibility
All pregnant GitLab GK team members
Available regardless of employment length
Key Features
Leave starts 6 weeks before due date. Government provides 2/3 salary during entire leave period.
How to Access
Plan: Leave begins 6 weeks before delivery
Apply: Submit government allowance application
Coordinate: Work with manager and People Operations
Important Information
May coordinate with GitLab Parental Leave if eligible
Parental Leave (Child Care Leave)
Overview
Take up to one year of protected leave with substantial government financial support.
Eligibility
Available until child’s first birthday
Fixed-term employees need one year employment
Must commit to return to work
Key Features
Receive 67% salary (first 6 months), 50% thereafter. Both parents can take leave.
How to Access
Plan: Starts after maternity leave (mothers) or from birth (fathers)
Apply: Submit allowance application
Coordinate: Arrange role coverage
Important Information
May coordinate with GitLab Parental Leave if eligible
Additional Family Support
Child Nursing Care Leave: Up to 5 days (1 child) or 10 days (2+ children). Runs with GitLab PTO.
Family Care Leave: Up to 10 days annually for sick/injured family members. Runs with GitLab
PTO.
Nursing Care Leave: Up to 93 days for family member nursing care, with potential government
allowance.
Time Off & Leave Policies
Annual/Vacation Leave
Overview
Guaranteed annual leave starting at 10 days after six months, increasing to maximum 20 days.
