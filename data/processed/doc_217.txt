Premiums and Taxability (Benefit in Kind)
GitLab pays 100% of the dental premium for you and your dependents. Employer-paid dental
premiums are a Benefit in Kind (BIK) under Revenue rules. BIK is calculated on the gross
premium, and the related tax is deducted through payroll.

Financial Security
Pension Plan
GitLab offers a defined contribution pension scheme through Aviva, administered by NFP Ireland,
designed to help you build financial security for retirement. Effective 2025-11-01, all new hires will
be automatically enrolled under our company plan.
NEW (January 2026) - Pension Auto Enrolment Regulation 
Starting January 1, 2026, Ireland’s new Auto Enrolment pension scheme (called “My
Future Fund”) becomes mandatory. All eligible employees who are not already
contributing to a workplace pension scheme will be automatically enrolled in this
government-run pension program.
Opportunity: Team Members can join our superior company DC pension plan and avoid
being automatically enrolled in the less beneficial government scheme.
Key Advantages of our Company Plan: 3-5% employer matching contribution
(compared to 1.5% employer contribution in Auto Enrolment’s first phase), complete
contribution flexibility, income tax relief, flexible investment options and no mandatory
waiting periods.
See the Ireland Auto Enrolment Pension Transition FAQ for detailed information
