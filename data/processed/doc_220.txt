Annual Health Checkup (Korea)
GitLab provides eligible team members in Korea with a company-supported comprehensive
health checkup every year. See the Korea Health Checkup Program Guide for full details.
Annual Check Up Requirements
Under Korea’s Occupational Safety and Health Act, employees are required to complete a health
exam arranged by the company.
Book your checkup as early as you can. Appointments and preferred locations fill up
toward year-end.
Complete your checkup by December 31 of the calendar year.
Email total-rewards@gitlab.com with your appointment date and location once
you’ve booked.
What's included and why it matters
Fully company-supported for eligible team members. Optional extra tests are at your
own cost, and you’ll see the final package and price before you confirm.
Early detection: regular screening helps catch health issues when they’re easiest to
address.
Your choice of date and location: 8 KMI centers nationwide plus partner hospitals.
Booking is online, and KMI appointments are confirmed immediately.
Your results are provided directly to you.
How to book
Go to www.vitabridge.co.kr (or use the Android app) and sign up or log in. Kakao, Naver,
and PASS logins work.
Select GitLab Korea. If it isn’t listed, choose Add and enter the company code (see page
7 of the Korea Health Checkup Program Guide).

Retirement Planning
National Pension Scheme
All GitLab Korea team members are automatically enrolled in Korea’s National Pension Scheme as
part of the Social Security Program, providing foundational retirement security.
Coverage & Key Features
Mandatory enrollment for all team members
Contributions shared between employer and employee
Portable benefits that follow you throughout your career
GitLab does not currently offer supplemental pension benefits, as the National Pension Scheme
provides comprehensive retirement coverage for all team members.
Growing Family Leaves
GitLab Korea provides comprehensive parental leave benefits that go beyond statutory
requirements, ensuring you have the time and financial security to focus on your family during
these important moments. The information below outlines the statutory entitlement for each
leave, team members may be eligible for GitLab Parental Leave policy in addition to their
statutory coverage.
Maternity Leave
Comprehensive maternity leave for all team members giving birth.
Eligibility Requirements
All team members giving birth
Includes coverage for miscarriage or stillbirth with scaled leave periods
Leave Entitlement
Standard Leave: 90 calendar days total
Multiple Births: Extended to 120 calendar days
Flexible Timing: Start date negotiable, but 45 days (60 for multiples) must follow
delivery

Paternity Leave
10 days of paid leave for new fathers to bond with their child and support their partner. If you are
eligible, you will receive 100% paid Parental Leave from GitLab up to 16 weeks.
Leave Entitlement
Statutory Entitlment: 10 calendar days to Use within 90 days of birth
Flexible Usage: Take consecutively or split into two periods
100% Paid: Full salary continuation through GitLab
Extended Parental Leave (Child Care Leave)
Up to one year of additional leave for ongoing childcare needs, available to eligible parents.
Extended leave of up to 18 months may be available in certain circumstances.
Eligibility Requirements
Minimum of six months of GitLab employment prior to scheduled leave start date
Child must be eight years old or younger, or in second grade or below in elementary
school
Pregnant employees may apply to protect their pregnancy
Parents cannot take leave simultaneously
Leave Entitlement
Duration: Up to one year per parent
Extended Duration: Up to 18 months total for parents who qualify under any of the
following:
Both parents have taken at least three months of childcare leave each for the same child
Single parents as defined under the Single-Parent Family Support Act
Parents of a child with a qualifying disability as prescribed by the Ministry of
Employment and Labor
Insurance Support: 40% salary from Employment Insurance Fund
GitLab Supplement: Additional support to reach 100% pay for up to 16 weeks (if eligible
under GitLab’s Parental Leave policy)
