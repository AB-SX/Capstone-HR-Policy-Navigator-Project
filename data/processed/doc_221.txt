Short-Term Child Care Leave - New as of August 2026
Effective 20 August 2026, team members may draw on their Child Care Leave entitlement in
shorter weekly increments to address short-term care gaps, rather than taking the full leave in
30-day increments.
This option is designed to accommodate situations such as school holidays, unexpected school or
daycare closures, or a child’s sudden illness — circumstances where a full month of leave is not
needed.
Leave Entitlement
Leave may be taken in 1-week or 2-week increments
This benefit can only be used once per calendar year
Time taken in weekly increments is deducted from your total unused Child Care Leave
entitlement (up to 1 year, or 1.5 years if applicable) — it is not additional leave
Eligibility conditions remain the same: child must be 8 years old or younger, or in second
grade or below
How to Apply
Provide advance notice as early as possible — 30 days is preferred where circumstances
allow
Contact the Absence Management team at leaves@gitlab.com
Fertility Treatment Leave
Up to six days per year for team members receiving fertility treatment such as artificial
insemination or in-vitro fertilization.
Eligibility Requirements
Available to all team members receiving qualifying fertility treatment
Leave Entitlement
Duration: Up to six days per year
Pay: First two days are paid; remaining days are unpaid
How to Request: Please provide advanced notice when possible. Medical
documentation may be required. You can submit a Compass case to the Absence
Management team for more information.
Family Care Leave
Leave to care for family members due to illness, accident, old age, or to care for a child.
Eligibility Requirements
Minimum six months of GitLab employment
Available for care of parent, grandparent, grandchild, child, spouse, or parent-in-law
Leave Entitlement
Short-term Family Care Leave: Up to 10 days per year (unpaid), can be split over
multiple occasions

Extended Family Care Leave: Up to 90 days per year (unpaid), including any short-term

How to Request: Please provide advanced notice when possible. You can submit a

Important Dates/Details
Advance Notice: 30 days required for all parental leave types
Documentation: Medical certificates required for maternity leave
Coordination: Work with Total Rewards team for insurance claims
Time Off Policies
Statutory Vacation
Team members with 0-1 year of service are entitled to 1 day/month or 12 days/year.
Team members with 1-3 years of service are entitled to 1.25 days/month or 15 days/year.
Team members with 3+ years of service are entitled to one additional day for every two
years continuously worked up to 25 days/year.
Accrual balances reset January 1st of every year.

Health & Wellbeing
Supplementary Medical Insurance
GitLab provides comprehensive supplementary medical insurance through AXA Health Insurance
to enhance your healthcare coverage beyond the French social security system. This valuable
coverage includes medical, vision, dental, and maternity benefits designed to support your health
and wellness needs.
