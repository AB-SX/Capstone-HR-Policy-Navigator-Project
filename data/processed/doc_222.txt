How to Enroll/Access
GitLab France S.A.S. team members can review statutory requirements for paid time off
in relation to vacation.
GitLab France S.A.S. team members should be aware of their applicable leave categories
and can review the requirements for logging time away from work and the suggested
order for using leave balances. (*internally accessible only)

which can be accessed via Okta, mobile and Slack.
For any support or inquiries relating to Workday absence management or leave
balances please open a Compass ticket with the People Operations team, who will
support or direct the query as needed.

from PTO. Team members are responsible for ensuring that they are tracking and
categorising their time accurately in Workday. When entering RTT time, team members
should select the RTT time off category in Workday.

Financial Security
Holiday Bonus
GitLab provides an annual Holiday Bonus to recognize your contributions and support your
financial wellbeing. This valuable benefit adds meaningful value to your total compensation
package.
Eligibility Requirements
All GitLab France S.A.S. team members
Must be employed during June to receive bonus
Based on prior 12 months of employment (June 1 - May 31)
Key Features
1% of total gross compensation from previous 12 months
Includes base salary, bonuses, commissions, and overtime
Subject to social charges and income tax
Paid automatically - no enrollment required
Payment Details
Payment date: June payroll each year
Calculation period: June 1 through May 31
Retirement Planning
While GitLab doesn’t currently offer supplementary pension benefits, all team members are
automatically enrolled in France’s comprehensive Social Security Program, including the National
Pension Scheme, providing retirement security aligned with French standards.
Growing Family Leaves
GitLab France offers comprehensive parental leave benefits that meet or exceed statutory
requirements. Whether you’re welcoming your first child or growing your family, we provide the
time and financial support you need during this important life transition.

Paternity Leave
Eligibility Requirements
All team members after birth of child
Includes births to spouse, common-law partner, or PACS partner
Must submit leave request 30 days in advance
Leave Entilement
Total leave: 28 days (35 days for multiple births)
Structure: 3 days mandatory at birth + 4 days immediately following + 21 additional days
21-day period can be split into two segments (minimum 5 days each)
GitLab pays 100% salary for first 3 days

Additional Birth Leave (congé supplémentaire de naissance)
Effective 1 July 2026 — Applies to children born or adopted on or after 1 January 2026. After
exhausting their statutory Maternity, Paternity, or Adoption Leave, eligible team members may
take Additional Birth Leave to continue caring for their new child.
Eligibility Requirements
All team members who have exhausted their statutory Maternity, Paternity, or Adoption
Leave for a child born or adopted on or after 1 January 2026
No minimum length of service requirement
Leave Entitlement
Each parent is entitled to a maximum of 2 months of leave
This is an individual entitlement — it cannot be transferred to the other parent if unused
