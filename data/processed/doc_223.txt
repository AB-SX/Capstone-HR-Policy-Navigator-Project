Leave can be taken as a 2 month block, or may be split into two periods of at least one
month each
Leave must be taken within 9 months of birth or adoption
For children born between 1 January 2026 and 30 June 2026, the 9-month window
begins on 1 July 2026, meaning leave must be used by 31 March 2027
This leave is paid by the French social security system (indemnités journalières) at a degressive
rate:
Month 1: 70% of net reference salary
Month 2: 60% of net reference salary
The monthly benefit is capped at the monthly Social Security Ceiling (PASS), which is EUR
4,005 in 2026
How to Apply
Notify your manager and provide at least 1 month’s advance notice to the Absence
Management Team at leaves@gitlab.com, specifying your desired start date and
duration. If your Additional Birth Leave immediately follows Paternity or Adoption Leave,
the notice period is reduced to 15 days
If taking the leave in two separate periods, indicate this in your request along with the
planned dates
Apply for social security daily allowances (indemnités journalières) as applicable

Sick Leave Policy
When illness strikes, GitLab provides comprehensive sick leave support through a combination of
French social security benefits and company salary supplementation, ensuring financial stability
during health challenges.
Eligibility Requirements
First 6 months of sick leave:
150 hours worked in prior 3 months, OR
Salary of 1,015 times hourly SMIC in prior 6 months
Beyond 6 months:
12 months of social security contributions
600 hours worked in prior 12 months, OR
Income of 2,030 times hourly SMIC in prior 12 months
Coverage & Key Features
Up to 6 months sick leave (extendable based on eligibility)
GitLab supplements social security to provide 100% salary for first 25 working days
After 25 days: Social security benefit of 50% daily wage (max €50.58/day)
