081. GitLab Singapore Pte Ltd
GitLab Singapore Pte Ltd
Discover GitLab’s benefits for team members in Singapore
We’ve Got You Covered
At GitLab, we believe team member benefits should be simple to understand and there when you
need them. From life’s big moments to everyday questions, our comprehensive coverage gives
you clarity and confidence so you can focus on your work and life.
In addition to the local benefits listed below, all Singapore team members have access to the 
company-wide benefits available to all team members globally.
Health & Wellbeing
Medical Insurance
GitLab Singapore team members are enrolled in comprehensive medical insurance benefits
through HSBC Insurance.
Coverage & Key Features
Hospital and Surgical - Coverage for inpatient procedures and hospital stays
Major Medical - Protection for significant medical expenses
Maternity - Support for growing families
Outpatient General Practitioner - Everyday medical care when you need it
Outpatient Specialist - Access to specialized medical expertise
Dental - Coverage for oral health needs
See the GitLab Singapore Benefit Overview for more details.
How to Access Specialist Care
Step 1: Check if your specialist is in the IHP Panel Clinics Directory. Please check the IHP App for
the most up to date list.
Step 2:
If your specialist is in the panel: Contact IHP at +64 6715 9422 before your
appointment for cashless arrangement
If your specialist is not in the panel: Obtain a specialist memo with diagnosis and
submit with your claim through the IHP portal

How to Enroll
New Team Members: You’ll receive a Singapore Benefits Onboarding Form during your
first week. Once completed, our Total Rewards team works to enroll you in all group
plans. Expect underwriting forms within your first month, if applicable.
Life Changes: Email total-rewards@gitlab.com with updates. When adding dependents,
include: Dependent’s full name, Date of birth, NRIC number, Gender Nationality.

Annual Health Screening
GitLab provides all Singapore team members with an annual Executive Health Screening
(Premium Scan) through Fullerton Health. This comprehensive screening helps you stay on top of
your health with preventive care.
What's Included
Executive Health Screening at any Fullerton Health location in Singapore
Direct billing to GitLab (not CPF payable)
Option to add tests or upgrade at your own cost
Benefit renews each calendar year
How to Book Your Screening
Visit the Fullerton Health Website
Click “Sign Up” and complete your information
Search for “GITLAB SINGAPORE PTE LTD” in the company dropdown
Review the Fullerton Health Guide for locations and screening details
Contact Information
Contact Fullerton Health at ehs@fullertonhealth.com or +65 6333 3636 (Option 3)
Financial Security
Group Term Life Insurance
GitLab provides company-paid Group Term Life Insurance through HSBC Insurance
Free Cover Limit (FCL): $300,000 (coverage above FCL is subject to underwriting and
acceptance).
Full coverage: 36 X Basic Monthly Salary, min $500,000, max $3 million
See the Group Term Life insurance section of the GitLab Singapore Benefit Overview for
complete details
Underwriting Guide
If your Life Insurance benefit surpasses the Free Cover Limit, HSBC will reach out to you to
conduct underwriting. See slide 18 of the GitLab Singapore Benefit Overview for our underwriting
guide.
Personal Accident Insurance
GitLab provides company-paid Group Personal Accident Policy through Zurich at 36 X Basic
Monthly Salary, min $500,000, max $3 million
Additional Protection includes:
Ambulance cost coverage
Emergency travel expenses
Trauma counseling support
