Retirement Planning
Singapore’s Central Provident Fund (CPF) provides comprehensive retirement security. While
GitLab doesn’t offer supplementary private pension benefits, the CPF system ensures your long-
term financial wellbeing. Learn more at cpf.gov.sg.
Growing Family Leaves
GitLab Singapore combines statutory leave with our global parental leave policy to provide
comprehensive support for growing families.
Maternity Leave
Team members employed for at least 3 months before birth may be eligible for the maternity
leaves listed below.
Government-Paid Maternity Leave (16 weeks)
1st and 2nd births: 8 weeks employer-paid + 8 weeks government-paid
3rd+ births: 16 weeks government-paid
First 8 weeks must be continuous; remaining weeks flexible within 12 months of birth
Eligible team members receive concurrent GitLab Parental Leave
Employment Act Maternity Leave (12 weeks)
8 weeks paid + 4 weeks unpaid
With GitLab Parental Leave, eligible team members receive up to 16 weeks paid
How to Apply
Submit “Parental Leave” request in Workday at least 30 days before leave
Send GPML Declaration Form to leaves@gitlab.com
GitLab handles government reimbursement claims
Paternity Leave
Team members employed for at least 3 months before due date may receive:
4 weeks government-paid paternity leave
Up to 16 weeks total when combined with GitLab Parental Leave
for eligible team members
How to Apply
Submit “Parental Leave” request in Workday
Send GPPL Declaration Form to leaves@gitlab.com
Learn more about eligibility at the Ministry of Manpower website.

Shared Parental Leave
Team members whose child’s date of birth, Estimated Delivery Date (EDD), or Formal Intent to
Adopt Date (FIAD) is on or after 1 April 2026 are eligible for 10 weeks of government-paid SPL, in
addition to the existing Maternity and Paternity Leave entitlements above.
10 weeks shared between both parents (5 weeks each by default; reallocation available
via the LifeSG portal)
Fully government-paid, capped at SGD 2,500 per week including CPF contributions
Must be used within 12 months of the child’s birth
Learn more about eligibility at the Ministry of Manpower website.
How to Apply
Provide at least 4 weeks’ notice before commencing a continuous block of SPL
Request “Shared Parental Leave” by emailing leaves@gitlab.com
