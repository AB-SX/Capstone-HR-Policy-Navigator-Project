Social Security Benefits
All workers in Mexico must be registered with and contribute to social security institutions under
the Social Security Law.
Key Institutions
Mexican Institute of Social Security (IMSS): Administers medical services, child day
care, accident and sickness compensation, pregnancy benefits, and disability pensions
National Workers’ Housing Fund Institute (INFONAVIT): Provides subsidized housing,
loans, and the Retirement Savings Program (SAR), which offers retirement benefits at
age 65
Contribution Requirements Both employers and employees are required to contribute to social
security, with employers responsible for withholding employee contributions. These
contributions fund retirement pensions, health and maternity insurance, occupational risk
coverage, day-care, disability and life insurance, and unemployment/old age insurance.

Sick Leave
Team members who cannot work due to nonwork-related injuries or illnesses may be eligible for
paid sick leave through the Social Security Institute.
Eligibility Requirements To qualify, you must have contributed to the social security
system for at least four weeks prior to when your medical condition developed.
Payment amount: 60% of your regular wages
Waiting period: Benefits begin on the fourth day of illness
Initial duration: Up to 52 weeks of coverage
Extension possibility: Benefits may be extended for an additional 52 weeks
Coverage Scope This program covers injuries and illnesses that are not related to your
work duties and are administered through the Social Security Institute.

Philippine Social Security Benefits
Team members and employers in the Philippines contribute to three mandatory social security
systems:
Social Security System (SSS) Provides pension, unemployment, maternity, disability,
sickness, death, and funeral benefits through joint employer and employee
contributions.
Home Development Mutual Fund (Pag-IBIG) A provident savings system offering
affordable housing financing to employed and self-employed members.
Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) A government corporation
under the Department of Health that provides health insurance coverage and ensures
affordable, accessible healthcare services for all members.
