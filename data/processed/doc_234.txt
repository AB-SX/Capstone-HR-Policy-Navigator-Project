Poland Leave Policies
Annual Leave
Entitlement: minimum 20 days per year. Employees with 10+ years of combined work/education
seniority are entitled to 26 days.

Education Level Years Added
Basic vocational education Up to 3 years
Secondary vocational education Up to 5 years
High school 4 years
Post-secondary school 6 years
Higher education (university) 8 years
A university graduate starts with 8 years’ seniority and gains the right to 26 days after 2 years of
work. Employers must ensure at least 14 consecutive days of leave are taken. Employees must
submit a vacation plan for the full tax year.
Public Holidays
13 paid public holidays per year. No extra pay if a holiday falls on a Sunday (no substitute day off).
If a holiday falls on a scheduled day off, the employee may take a substitute day in the future.
Sick Leave
Period Pay
First 33 days (employer-funded) 80% of salary
After 33 days (ZUS-funded) 80% of base salary
Employees aged 50+ Employer pays only first 14 days, ZUS covers the rest
Pregnancy / work accident 100% of salary (ZUS covers the difference)
Maximum paid sick leave: 182 days per calendar year.
Parental Leave
Maternity
Number of Children Leave Duration

Paid at 100% of salary by ZUS. Leave may begin up to 6 weeks before the due date. At least 14
weeks must be taken immediately after birth. Remaining leave may be transferred to the father.
Breastfeeding breaks: two 30-minute breaks/day if working 6+ hours; one 30-minute break if
working fewer than 6 hours.
Paternity
Up to 2 weeks, to be taken within the first 12 months after birth (or before the adopted child
turns 7). Can be split into two periods of at least 1 week each. Paid at 100% by ZUS.
Parental
41 weeks (43 for multiple births) available after maternity leave is fully used. Can be taken by one
parent or split between both.

Period Pay
First 6 weeks (8 for multiples, 3 for older adopted child) 100%, covered by ZUS
Remaining weeks 60%, covered by ZUS
Up to 4 separate periods, each at least 8 weeks. Must be used before the end of the calendar year
in which the child turns 6.
Other Leave
Disability leave: Additional 10 working days per year for employees with a disability
Carer’s leave: 5 unpaid days per year to care for a family member with a serious
medical condition. Social security earnings suspended during use.
Study leave: Paid leave for education programmes initiated by, or with permission from,
the employer. Duration varies by programme.
Extramural exams: 6 days for professional qualification confirmation; 21 days for
diploma dissertation and exam.
Special events: 2 days for family life events (wedding, childbirth, funeral); 1 day for a
child’s wedding or death of an in-law. Special cases also cover court appearances,
mandatory medical exams, and blood donation.
Unpaid leave: Employee may request for any reason; employer may decline. If longer
than 3 months, employer may ask the employee to return to work for important
reasons.
Force Majeure Leave: 2 days, 50% paid by employer for urgent family emergencies
(again, they would be eligible for GitLab’s Caregiver Sick Leave / Bereavement /
Emergency leave policies which are more generous)
Special Occasion Leave: 2 days which can be used for own marriage or relative’s
marriage, or death of a close relative. Fully paid and does not reduce annual leave
entitlement.
Blood Donation Leave: 1 working day per donation, full pay.
