Sick Leave
Under Section 22(1) to 22(4) of South Africa’s Employment Act, employees are entitled to paid sick
leave during each 36-month cycle beginning from their first day of employment.
Calculation Method: Sick leave entitlement equals the number of days an employee
typically works during a 6-week period:

UAE Leave Policies
Annual Leave
After 1 Year: 30 calendar days/22 working days
First Year: 2 days per month after 6 months of service
Part-Time: Prorated based on actual working hours
Parental Leave
Private sector team members are entitled to 5 paid working days of parental leave, available to
both mothers and fathers. This leave can be taken from the day of the child’s birth up to six
months after delivery.
Maternity Leave
Female workers receive 60 days of maternity leave structured as follows:
45 days: Fully paid leave
15 days: Half-paid leave (during probation period only)
Application Timing: To initiate parental leave, submit your time off by selecting parental leave
category in Workday - Time Off at least 30 days before the leave starts. Please familiarize yourself
with GitLabs Parental leave policy.
Additional Maternity Provisions:
Pregnancy/childbirth complications: Additional 45 days unpaid leave available if
unable to return to work due to illness resulting from pregnancy or childbirth. Medical
certificate from respective medical authority required. Days may be taken consecutively
or intermittently.
Sick or disabled child: Additional 30 days fully paid leave, extendable for another 30
days unpaid. Medical certificate from respective medical authority required to prove
child’s illness or disability.
