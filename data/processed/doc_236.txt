Nursing breaks: After returning to work, entitled to one or two daily breaks for nursing
(maximum one hour total per day) for 6 months following delivery. These breaks are
fully paid.
Sabbatical Leave (National Service)
Under UAE Labour Law, Emirati citizens working in the private sector are entitled to paid
sabbatical leave to perform national service.
Sick Leave
Eligibility: Available after completing the probationary period. Maximum 90 days per year
(continuous or intermittent).
Payment Structure:
First 15 days: Full pay
Next 30 days: Half pay
Remaining 45 days: No pay
Probationary Period: Sick leave available without pay, subject to employer approval and medical
report confirming necessity.
Notification Requirements: Must notify employer within 3 days of illness and submit medical
report from medical entity (per Article 31 of UAE Labour Law).
Ineligibility Situations:
During probation period (for paid leave)
Illness resulting from employee misconduct (alcohol/narcotics consumption)
Illness resulting from violation of safety instructions per UAE legislation and company
regulations
Note: Above provisions do not apply to occupational illness-related sick leave.
Special Leave
Bereavement: 5 days (spouse), 3 days (parent/child/sibling/grandparent/grandchild)
Hajj: Up to 30 days unpaid, once per employment period
National Service: Paid sabbatical for Emiratis
Application Process
For Parental Leave: Submit through Time Off by Deel, select Parental Leave label. Absence
Management team will provide additional information.
For All Leave Types: Submit requests via Workday - Time Off at least 30 days before leave starts.

084. Leave Types
Leave Types
On this page you will find GitLab’s leave policies, including GitLab Parental Leave, Emergency
Leave, Military Leave, Sick Leave, and Caregiving Sick Leave.
GitLab Parental Leave
GitLab’s paid Parental Leave is available for any active team member (regardless of gender) who
becomes a parent through childbirth or adoption while working at GitLab. The information in this
section is specific to GitLab’s 16-week paid Parental Leave. Different countries or US states may provide
team members with their own statutory Parental Leave benefits. Please review your country/state
Parental Leave benefits for more information on how to apply and tenure requirements (if applicable).
Eligibility, Entitlement and Planning Your Leave
GitLab’s Parental Leave is available for any active team member (regardless of gender) who
becomes a parent through childbirth, adoption, fostering, or surrogacy (intended parent) while
working at GitLab. This benefit is available from your first day of employment, provided your child
was born, newly adopted, newly placed in your foster care, or born via surrogacy during your
employment at GitLab.
GitLab provides 16 weeks of paid Parental Leave, which must be used prior to the child’s first
birthday, the first anniversary of the adoption, the first anniversary of the foster placement, or
the first anniversary of the birth (for surrogacy). Team members are encouraged to decide for
themselves how much time they’d like to take, but there are some parameters to keep in mind
when planning your leave:
We encourage you to begin your leave when your child arrives to maximize time with
your family. If you prefer to start on a different date, please connect with the Absence
Management team to review your request.
You may take your Parental Leave all at once or split it into up to three segments. Read
more here about how you should communicate and prepare for planned time away.
When calculating leave dates, GitLab uses a 1/7 formula (1 week = 7 days), meaning if
you return on a Monday, your leave ends on Sunday.
If your Parental Leave falls within the 4 scheduled Family and Friends Days, your
parental leave will be extended by 4 days.
FAQ: What happens with public holidays while I’m on Parental Leave?
GitLab’s Public Holiday policy provides each team member with a public holiday balance
to use flexibly to celebrate what’s important to them and must be used within that
calendar year. Unused public holidays will not rollover to the next calendar year while on
parental leave, unless statutorily required by law.

Coverage planning: We expect all team members taking parental leave to work with
their manager to develop a strong coverage plan before they start their leave (when

As you prepare for your parental leave, please note the following:
No Work Required During Your Leave: You are not required to log into any GitLab
systems, review or approve compensation documents, or respond to any work-related
emails or deadlines.
Automated System Emails: You may receive automated emails from our systems
during your leave. Please disregard these emails - they do not require any action from
you.
Approvals: Any required approvals will be handled by the team or when you return from
leave. You will not be disadvantaged for being unable to complete approvals during your
protected leave period, and your compensation components will be preserved and
addressed appropriately upon your return.
If you have questions before your leave begins, please contact leaves@gitlab.com.
Supplement with Paid Time Off
If you need additional time after your Parental Leave:
You may utilize GitLab’s Flexible Paid Time Off policy with manager coordination. If
business critical deadlines will be missed, your Manager may ask you to change the
dates.
Discuss this with your manager following standard PTO guidelines (double the amount
of notice as time requested).
Submit PTO dates in Workday by selecting “Paid Time Off” and notify the Absence
Management team.
You may not extend paid parental leave through the use of Paid Caregiver Sick Time, unless you
have a family member with a serious health condition.
How to Initiate Your Leave
Share when comfortable: You can share your news with your manager whenever you
feel ready, but we recommend communicating 2-3 months before your leave starts for
workload planning.
30-45 days prior to leave: remember to review your location’s reporting requirements!
USA and Canada: Please submit your Parental Leave request to Tilt (accessed through Okta).
