People Analytics Office Hours
Overview
People Analytics Office Hours provide a regular, accessible forum where GitLab team members
can receive direct support and guidance from the People Analytics team. These sessions are
designed to democratize data access, improve data literacy across the organization, and enhance
collaboration between the People Analytics team and business stakeholders.
Purpose
To create an open, supportive environment where team members can:
Get immediate assistance with data-related questions
Receive hands-on analytics support
Learn about available People Analytics resources and services
Collaborate on potential analysis opportunities
Build confidence in data utilization and interpretation
What We Cover
Data Analysis Support
Troubleshooting data questions and interpretation challenges
Guidance on statistical approaches and methodology
Help with data validation and quality assessment
Support for advanced analytics requests
Chart selection and design principles
Tableau Dashboard Assistance
Navigation tips and best practices for existing dashboards
Custom view creation and filtering techniques
Understanding metric definitions and calculations
Troubleshooting access issues and permissions

Session Details
Schedule & Format
Frequency: Every 2 weeks (alternating time zones)
Duration: 30 minutes
Format: Zoom call
Agenda: Notes and conversation will be documented in the agenda
Timing Options
To accommodate GitLab’s global workforce, we alternate between different time zones:
Rotation 1: Wednesdays, 8:30 AM PT / 11:30 AM ET / 3:30 PM UTC
Rotation 2: Wednesdays, 11:00 AM PT / 2:00 PM ET / 6:00 PM UTC
How to Participate
Before the Session
Submit Questions: If possible, add your questions or topics to the agenda ahead of
time. If not, no worries, just show up with your questions or discussion topics
Prepare Materials: If you have specific dashboards, reports, or data you’d like to
discuss, have them ready to screen share
Review Resources: Check our People Analytics Tableau Dashboard Overview if your
question relates to existing dashboards
During the Session
Questions are typically addressed in the order they appear on the agenda
Feel free to ask questions that come to mind in the moment

After the Session
If more support is needed, feel free to set up time with the People Analytics team or

Getting Help Between Sessions
While Office Hours provide regular touchpoints, don’t hesitate to reach out between sessions for:
Urgent Requests: Use our People Analytics project issue templates
Quick Questions: Reach out directly through Slack in the #people-analytics channel
Complex Projects: Schedule dedicated sync meeting to talk through your needs
Resources & Preparation
Recommended Reading
People Analytics Data Guide - Understand our data models and metrics, specifically our 
Data Dictionary
People Analytics Tableau Dashboard Overview - Familiarize yourself with available

Expected Outcomes
We strive to ensure these sessions are a valuable use of time. We track the effectiveness of Office
Hours through:
Impact Measurement
Reduction in individual support requests through email/Slack
Improvement in Tableau dashboard usage metrics - monitored on this dashboard
Follow-up project requests generated from sessions - tracked with the PA-office-hours
label in our project tracker
Knowledge Transfer Indicators
Repeat question patterns (decreasing indicates improved understand and usage of
resources)
Complexity evolution of submitted questions
Self-service success rates for common tasks - less attendance is an indication most
questions are being answered without the need to reach out
