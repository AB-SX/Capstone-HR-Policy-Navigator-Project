Accessibility & Accommodations Support
At GitLab, everyone belongs and everyone can contribute. As a new team member, we want to
ensure you have the tools, accommodations, and support you need to thrive from day one. We’re
here to remove barriers and create an environment where you can bring your best self to work.
Global Accessibility and Accommodations Policy
Note: To ensure proper support and avoid declined requests, any equipment beyond our
standard offerings requires approval before purchasing.
Technology & Equipment Setup
Standard Equipment: All GitLab team members can access ergonomic equipment
through an office budget stipend, and technology through our regular IT processes.
Accommodations & Adjustments: Assistive technology or specialized equipment
beyond standard offerings can be requested through our Team Member Relations (TMR)
team (teammemberrelations@gitlab.com).

Your First Steps
Before Day 1: If you need workplace accommodations or adjustments beyond standard
equipment, connect with our Team Member Relations team
(teammemberrelations@gitlab.com) for assistance.
Day 1-3: Explore accessibility features in your GitLab tools (Slack, Zoom, Google
Workspace).
First Week: For standard ergonomic equipment, discuss with your manager and IT. For
specialized accommodations, submit a request in Compass (internal system) for the
Team Member Relations team.
Ongoing: Use standard IT processes for standard equipment needs, and reach out to
the Team Member Relations (TMR) team via Compass (internal system) to request
ongoing accommodations and/or adjustments.

New Hire FAQ: Everything You Need to Know
Equipment and Expenses
Q: Can I purchase work items before my start date and get reimbursed later?
A: We recommend waiting until your start date, as you’ll receive a virtual card with funds for
equipment purchases. If you do make purchases before starting, email expenses@gitlab.com
immediately, as these will count against your onboarding funds.
Q: What if my GitLab laptop hasn’t arrived by my first day?
A: You’re welcome to use a personal laptop temporarily. Just postpone any hardware-related
security setup tasks until your GitLab laptop arrives.
Q: How do I get reimbursed for a laptop I purchased for work?
A: You’ll gain access to Navan (our expense system) during your first week. Submit your receipts
through this platform for reimbursement.
Q: Can I work from a co-working space instead of home?
A: Absolutely! Just review our co-working space guidelines once you get started in the internal
handbook before signing any agreements.
Q: Does GitLab provide funds for home office equipment?
A: Yes! All new team members receive $1,500 USD (or local equivalent) via a virtual card for office
equipment purchases.
Payroll & Benefits
Q: When will my first paycheck be?
A: After your date of hire you can check the payroll cut-off dates in our internal handbook to
determine your first pay date. Please note the internal handbook can only be accessed once
you have started at GitLab. If you’re hired after the cut-off date, the prorated amount will be
included in the following month’s paycheck. If you have any questions prior to your start date on
the payroll cut off in respect to your start date, please reach out to us via 
people_operations@gitlab.com.
Q: How should I handle pre-planned vacations?
A: Discuss any pre-planned time off with your manager as soon as possible to ensure proper
coverage, then enter the time in Workday (you will have access once you get started with us).
