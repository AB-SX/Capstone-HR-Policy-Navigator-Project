Diversity, Inclusion & Belonging at GitLab
Diversity, Inclusion & Belonging is fundamental to the success of GitLab. We include it in every
way possible and in all that we do. We strive for a transparent environment where all globally
dispersed voices are heard and welcomed. We strive for an environment where people can show
up as their full selves each day and can contribute to their best ability. And with over 100,000
organizations utilizing GitLab across the globe, we strive for a team that is representative of our
users.
Diversity, inclusion, and belonging are part of our Culture of Excellence, the foundation of our
Operating Principles.
And diversity in our leadership supports innovation, promotes better decision making and 
improves financial results.
GitLab’s Diversity Inclusion and Belonging Mission
Mission:We create spaces where everyone belongs and deliver programs so that everyone can
contribute.
At GitLab, Diversity, Inclusion & Belonging (DIB) is fundamental to our success. We are building
an environment where everyone can thrive and contribute through three strategic pillars:
Pillar 1: Empowering our people
Growing our talent & their experience through comprehensive learning and
development programs
Equipping team members with skills-based learning, career mobility resources, and
tailored learning journeys
Supporting TMRGs (Team Member Resource Groups) and TMAGs (Team Member
Advocacy Groups)
Implementing leadership development through our Elevate programs
Pillar 2: Uplifting our communities
Engaging with external communities through strategic partnerships and sponsorships
Supporting DIB-focused events like Women of Silicon Roundabout, Black Techfest, and
Grace Hopper Celebration
Giving back through volunteer opportunities and nonprofit donations
Advancing our mission through the GitLab Foundation
