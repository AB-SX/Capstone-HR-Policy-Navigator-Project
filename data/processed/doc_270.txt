Career Development and Mobility
Resources and direction for career development and mobility for all GitLab team members
What is Career Development
Career development is the non-linear, lifelong growth process integrating learning, work, leisure,
and change. It happens in all directions, including subject matter expertise, lateral growth to new
teams, and linear growth through a job family. Use the resources on this page to navigate your
career growth at GitLab.
