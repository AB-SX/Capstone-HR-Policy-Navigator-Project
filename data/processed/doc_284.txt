107. GitLab IT Onboarding 101
GitLab IT Onboarding 101
Step-by-step guide for new hires to activate Okta, set up their GitLab Mac or Linux computer, and
access their Google and GitLab accounts.
Welcome To GitLab
Congratulations on joining GitLab! This page is dedicated to the IT onboarding experience, here
at GitLab we use many applications and the latest hardware to enable you to complete your work
to the best of your ability! While it may seem a little bit overwhelming at the start, we are here to
make the process as easy as possible!
IMPORTANT: You will not have access to your work Mac until your start date. On your start
date, please follow this guide.

Activate Your Okta Account
You will receive an Okta activation email in your personal email. This is the first step to gaining
access to GitLab’s systems.
On a mobile device, check your personal email for an Okta activation email.
Click the link in the email to set your password and configure Okta Verify on your mobile
device.
After setting up your account, your browser will redirect to the Okta dashboard.
IMPORTANT: Either Okta Verify or an enrolled FIDO2 key (like a YubiKey) is required to set up
your GitLab-managed Mac. Make sure either is set up during your Okta account onboarding.
