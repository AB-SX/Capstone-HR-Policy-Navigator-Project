Once you land at the desktop, GitLab’s onboarding app (Exit0) opens the Set up your
Mac window. Click Start setup, or wait 10 seconds for it to start on its own. It installs
your core apps (Google Chrome, Slack, etc.), applies required settings, and verifies
security compliance before use.
When the You’re all set! screen appears, setup is finished. If FileVault full disk
encryption is already enabled, your Mac is ready to use. If FileVault is not enabled, you
will be prompted to restart the Mac in order to enable it. Click Restart or wait for the 60-
second countdown, then log back in with your local Mac account password.
If setup stops or shows an error, email it-help@gitlab.com from your personal email address.
Manual Mac Setup
Manual enrollment is only for Macs that cannot use automated enrollment, such as a Mac that
was not purchased through GitLab’s authorized vendors. Manually enrolling Macs should be a
last resort and is not considered a standard process. If you live in a country where GitLab is not
able to ship a Mac to you, email it-help@gitlab.com to confirm it’s the right option.
Before you begin, make sure your Mac is running macOS 15 or later and you can sign in to it with
an administrator account.
Email it-help@gitlab.com and ask for access to manual Mac enrollment. IT adds you to
the Okta group for manual enrollment and lets you know when it’s done.
On your Mac, open Safari. Google Chrome isn’t installed yet.
