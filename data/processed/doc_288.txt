Set Up Okta Verify on Mac
Okta Verify is installed automatically during Mac setup and is the primary way to authenticate to
apps used at GitLab. The easiest way to set it up is to import the Okta Verify account from your
mobile device.
Before you begin:
Keep your mobile device near your Mac, with Bluetooth turned on for both.
