Learning Initiatives Introduction
The Learning & Development team is rolling out learning programs to enable a culture of
curiosity and continual learning. The learning initiatives are intended to provide a forum for team
members to come together to learn about all of the activities taking place in the learning space,
as well as hear from leaders internally and externally on various leadership topics.

120. Team Workshops
Team Workshops
Team Workshops are customized, group learning opportunities for teams at GitLab. Workshops are
interactive, collaborative, and led by expert facilitators. They are designed to address specific skill
gaps, create space and time for building connection and trust, and developing shared best
practices.
The program is available to all teams at GitLab, including teams of individual contributors and
people leaders. Workshop duration is varied.
Currently offered workshops cover the following skills:
Having Difficult Conversations
Executive Presence and Influence
Storytelling
Building Trust and Resiliency
Leading through Change
Inclusive Leadership
Open and Effective Communication
Decision Making and Managing Ambiguity
Unconscious Bias
These skills were selected and prioritized based on themes derived from the talent development
listening tour feedback from People Business Partners, and Elevate Programs.
When you’re ready to start planning, open an issue to connect with the Talent Development team
for support.
