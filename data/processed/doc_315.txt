Tax Documents: ACA Tax Form 1095-C: Health Coverage Statements & How to Request
IMPORTANT NOTICE: This information explains how to access your ACA Form 1095-C, a tax
statement that reports the health coverage GitLab offered to you as a full-time employee.
GitLab no longer automatically mails paper 1095-C forms. This change is due to the Paperwork
Burden Reduction Act (PBRA) enacted in December 2024.
Getting Your Form Online
Your 1095-C tax form is available through PlanSource:
Log into PlanSource
Go to Documents > ACA Documents > List of your documents by years
Select the tax year you need, tax documents will be available to download no later than
March 2nd
Requesting a Paper Copy by Mail
You can opt in to receiving a physical 1095-C form at any time by:
Logging into PlanSource and clicking “I want to receive my 1095-C by mail” on the ACA
Documents screen, OR
Contacting Total Rewards using any method below
Contact Total Rewards
Email: total-rewards@gitlab.com
Mail: GitLab Inc., 268 Bush Street #350, San Francisco, CA 94104
Phone: (408) 712-3237

Getting Started with Your Benefits
You have 30 days from your start date to sign up for benefits through PlanSource (our benefits
system).
Important: If you don’t choose a plan within 30 days, you’ll automatically get the Medical
Waiver Allowance and won’t have other optional coverage.
For help understanding how benefits work, check out the PlanSource Video Library. You can also
find payroll deduction details on our Accounting and Reporting page.
Who Can Get Benefits?
You’re eligible if you’re a full-time GitLab team member working at least 30 hours per week. Your
benefits start on your first day.
You can also cover:
Your spouse or domestic partner
Your children up to age 26 (including adopted and stepchildren)
Adult children with disabilities who can’t support themselves
Important Notes & Domestic Partner Details
If you and an eligible family member both work at GitLab, you can only be covered once
under our plans
For domestic partners: You’ll need to complete a Domestic Partner Affidavit (except
during new hire or open enrollment). Domestic partner coverage may be taxable income
and premium contributions may not be pre-tax eligible under Section 125
How to Enroll

Qualifying Life Events
You can make benefit changes outside of open enrollment when you experience qualifying life
events such as:
Marriage or divorce
Birth or adoption of a child
Spouse’s employment changes
Moving to a different state
Process: Log into PlanSource within 30 days of your qualifying event to submit changes with. For
step-by-step instructions on how to submit a Qualifying Life Event (QLE) in PlanSource (including
how to add dependents and upload documentation), see the Qualifying Life Event (QLE) Process
& Documentation guide
Health & Wellbeing: Complete Wellness Support
Medical Coverage
GitLab provides medical insurance through Cigna nationwide, with Kaiser options available for
California and Hawaii residents. If you already have coverage elsewhere, you can opt out and
receive a monthly allowance instead.
Need Help Choosing? Use the Flimp PlanSelect tool to find the right plan for you, or contact
Cigna One Guide Service for personalized assistance walking through your options.
Medical Waiver Allowance
Already have group medical coverage? You can opt out and receive a $150 monthly benefit
allowance while still enrolling in dental, vision, and other benefits. You must actively waive
medical coverage in PlanSource to receive this allowance. If you do not enroll in a plan within your
benefits election period, you will automatically be enrolled in the Medical Waiver Allowance.
