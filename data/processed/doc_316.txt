Voluntary Accident, Hospital, and Critical Illness Insurance
GitLab offers three voluntary insurance plans with our benefit carrier, Cigna: Critical Illness,
Accident Insurance, and Hospital Indemnity Insurance. These are all voluntary benefits, meaning
enrollment is optional and there is an after-tax premium if you choose to enroll.
Team Members have the opportunity to enroll during New Hire Enrollment, Open Enrollment, or
if they experience a Qualifying Life Event.
See the US Benefits Guide for plan details and costs.

Evidence of Insurability & Leavers
If you choose supplemental life insurance above the guaranteed amount, you or your dependent
may need to complete an Evidence of Insurability Form (EOI). You’ll be prompted during
enrollment if this applies to you
If you leave GitLab: Supplemental life insurance isn’t included in COBRA and ends on your last
day. You can continue coverage directly with Cigna, contact them for premium information and
setup.
