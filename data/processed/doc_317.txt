How to apply for Short-Term Disability
If you’re unable to work due to a qualifying disability, contact the Absence Management team at 
leaves@gitlab.com as soon as possible. See Sick Time reporting procedures
The process:
The Absence Management team will guide you through the application process. This will
include:
a. Providing you with filing options (online portal, phone, or paper)
b. Providing NYLife with the employer eligibility intake information
c. Sending you the Assignment of Benefits form
After 26 weeks (maximum benefit period), you’ll need to determine if you can return to
work
Returning to work: Email leaves@gitlab.com a physician’s Return to Work authorization at least
5 calendar days before your planned return date. Include any needed accommodations.
If you can’t return after 26 weeks: You’ll move to unpaid leave with the option to continue
benefits through COBRA. You can also apply for long-term disability at this time.
NYLife Claim Process for Short-Term Disability
Step 1: Submit Your Short-Term Disability Application. Complete the claimant portion and
have your physician finish the rest. You can either:
Send directly to NYLife (using the mail/fax number on the form), or
Return to leaves@gitlab.com who will submit it for you
Let the Absence Management team know which option you choose.
Step 2: NYLife Review Process
Within 3 business days, NYLife will contact you if they need additional information
They may also contact your physician (with your authorization) and verify details with
Absence Management
Review begins immediately once they have your information
Step 3: Decision
If denied: NYLife will call to explain the decision, and Absence Management will process
any owed pay
If approved: Both you and Absence Management receive confirmation with your
approval date and estimated return-to-work date

Flexible Spending & Health Savings Accounts
Tax-advantaged accounts to help manage healthcare and dependent care costs:
Health Savings Account (HSA)
GitLab contributes to your HSA each month: $100 for individual coverage and $125 for family
coverage (team member + dependents). You must be enrolled in the High Deductible Health Plan
(HDHP) to be eligible for HSA contributions and enrollment. Please see the US Benefits Guide for
current year contribution limits.
HSA Details
Your HSA lets you set aside pre-tax money for qualified medical expenses, reducing your overall
healthcare costs.
Key benefits:
Tax advantages: Contributions reduce taxable income, earnings grow tax-free, and
withdrawals for medical expenses are tax-free
Flexible timing: Money rolls over annually and never expires—it’s yours forever
Investment options: Once your balance reaches the minimum, you can invest in
mutual funds, stocks, and bonds
Future flexibility: At age 65, withdraw for any purpose without penalty (medical
expenses remain tax-free)
Fidelity administers GitLab’s HSA plan. If you choose an HSA-eligible medical plan election in
PlanSource, PlanSource will guide you through the next steps to activate your HSA account with
Fidelity.
HSA Account Rollover & Administration
Transferring from a previous HSA: Contact Fidelity at 800-544-3716 or log in to Fidelity
NetBenefits to request a transfer form. Complete with your old provider’s account information,
Fidelity will handle the rest.
If you leave GitLab: Your HSA stays with you forever. You’ll become responsible for the monthly
administration fee once GitLab is no longer your employer.
Domestic Partner Coverage: If you’re not legally married, your domestic partner’s expenses
aren’t HSA-eligible. However, if they’re covered under your family HDHP, they can open their own
HSA. Both of you can contribute up to the full family maximum to your respective HSAs.
