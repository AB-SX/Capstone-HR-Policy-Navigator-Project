Health Savings Account and Offboarding GitLab
Your HSA and all funds are yours to keep when you leave GitLab. You can continue using the
money for eligible healthcare expenses at your next employer or let it grow over time.
Fidelity may charge management fees for direct account administration. Contact Fidelity at

Flexible Spending Accounts
FSAs let you pay for healthcare and dependent care expenses with pre-tax dollars. Set aside
money from each paycheck based on your estimated annual expenses. Changes only allowed
during open enrollment or qualifying life events. Please see the US Benefits Guide for current
year contribution limits.
Dependent Care FSA Covers childcare and eldercare expenses. Qualifying dependents:
children under 13, disabled spouse, or parents needing care. Claims incurred through
March 15, submitted by March 31.
Medical FSA Covers medical, dental, vision, and over-the-counter medications. Cannot
participate if you have an HSA.
Limited Purpose FSA Covers dental and vision only. Available if you have an HSA.
Rollover Details for Medical and Limited Purpose FSA
Rollover funds from prior year won’t be available until 5-15 business days after March 31 of the
current tax year (no later than April 22).
Contribution and Rollover Limits:
2026: $3,400 max contribution, $680 max rollover
Important Notes:
Minimum $5 required for rollover; amounts $5 or less are forfeited
You can use rollover funds the following year, even if you don’t enroll in FSA again
Flexible Spending Accounts and Leaving GitLab
Medical FSA: Your account terminates on your last day unless you continue through COBRA. You
can use your full FSA balance up to and including your last day.
COBRA option: Available if you’ve contributed more than you’ve spent
COBRA payments: Continue monthly contributions on post-tax basis
Claims deadline: Submit all claims within 90 days of termination

FSA Period to Submit Claims
You have 90 days after the plan year ends to submit claims (known as the runout period).
Mid-Year FSA Contribution Changes
You can only increase FSA contributions during a Qualifying Life Event; reductions aren’t allowed.
Additional funds can only be used for expenses incurred on or after your qualifying event date.
Example: If you originally elected $500 and increased it to $2,000 after getting married, you can
only be reimbursed up to $500 for expenses before your marriage date. The additional $1,500 is
available for expenses after your marriage.
Travel Health Reimbursement (HRA)
GitLab offers a Travel HRA benefit to reimburse transportation and lodging expenses when you
travel for medical care not available within 100 miles of your home.
You and your dependents must be enrolled in a Cigna Medical Plan. Navia Benefit Solutions
administers this benefit.
Reimbursement Amounts
Pre-tax account: Up to $2,100 for eligible travel expenses
Post-tax account: $900 for expenses exceeding the pre-tax limit
