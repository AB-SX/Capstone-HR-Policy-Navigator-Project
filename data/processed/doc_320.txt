Sick Leave
Sickness is fully covered by the employer in accordance with the applicable collective agreement

10 days of compulsory leave at 100% salary, to be taken within 5 months of the birth.
Parental leave
Up to 10–11 months of leave (partially paid). One month at 80% pay is available to either
parent within the child’s first 6 years.
