125. Financial Wellness
Financial Wellness
A summary of Financial Wellness resources available to team members at GitLab.
Financial Wellness: We’ve Got You Covered
Clear benefits, real support
Your financial wellbeing matters at GitLab. We provide comprehensive resources to support you
at every life stage—from understanding equity compensation to planning major transitions.
Whether you’re starting out, managing debt, growing a family, or planning retirement, our
benefits give you the tools and guidance to build financial confidence.
Note: GitLab provides resources and education but cannot offer direct financial advice.
Modern Health Financial Coaching
Access personalized financial guidance through Modern Health’s Employee Assistance Program.
Get practical strategies and understand the psychology of money management.
Eligibility
All GitLab team members globally
No cost, confidential support
Key Features
One-on-one financial coaching, digital money management programs, and group workshops
addressing financial decision-making psychology.
How to Access Modern Health Financial Coaching
Download Modern Health app or visit online platform
Add a Financial Coach to your care team
Access “Financial Well-being” digital program
Join Money Management Circles
Available Resources
One-on-One Coaching: Add a Financial Coach to your care team
Digital Programs: “Financial Well-being” app modules
Group Sessions: Modern Health Money Management Circle - Recording
Session Focus: Understanding wealth psychology, assessing your financial situation,
and identifying financial values
Questions?
Access support through the Modern Health platform.

SageView Financial Wellness Program
Dedicated financial wellness specialists help you understand your complete financial picture,
including GitLab equity plans. Focus areas include investment assets, asset protection, income
protection, debt management, and estate planning.
Eligibility
All GitLab team members worldwide
No cost to participate
Key Features
One-on-one consultations, educational workshops, and ongoing access to financial resources.
Main focus: understanding how your GitLab equity plan works.
How to Access
Schedule Consultation: Use scheduling flyer
Access Resources: Visit Sage411.com for general questions
Join Workshops: Attend Financial Wellness Workshops
Review Materials: Access regular SageView communications
Getting Started
Program Overview: Kickoff presentation or AMA session
Session Details: Program description
Important Information
SageView provides educational guidance but cannot offer specific tax, legal, or financial advice.
All consultations are confidential.
Questions?
Contact SageView through the scheduling system or total-rewards@gitlab.com.
