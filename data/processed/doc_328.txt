Italy Academic Study Tax-free. Under Art. 51(2)(f) and Art. 100 of the TUIR (D.P.R.
917/1986), education and instruction benefits provided to all
employees or homogeneous categories do not constitute
taxable income. Since GitLab’s G&D Fund is available to all team
members for educational purposes, academic study
reimbursements qualify for this exemption.
Professional Coaching Tax-free. Qualifies under two legal bases: (1) Art. 51(1) TUIR as
expense in the exclusive interest of the employer when directly
job-related; (2) Art. 51(2)(f) + Art. 100 TUIR as education benefit
when offered to all employees or homogeneous categories for
professional development purposes.
English Language
Courses
Tax-free. Language training qualifies as education/instruction
under Art. 51(2)(f) + Art. 100 TUIR.
Professional
Development/
Certifications/Licenses
Tax-free. Qualifies under two legal bases: (1) Art. 51(2)(f) + Art.
100 TUIR as education benefit offered to all employees; (2) Art.
51(1) TUIR as expense in the exclusive interest of the employer
when directly job-related (per Agenzia delle Entrate Risposta n.
421/2023 and Risoluzione n. 74/E/2017).
Professional Dues/
Membership
Tax-free if job-related. When membership is required or directly
useful for the employee’s role, it qualifies as an expense in the
exclusive interest of the employer under Art. 51(1) TUIR.

Tax-free. Online learning platforms qualify as education/
