How Support Handles Family and Friends Days
During Family and Friends Days GitLab Support continues
to operate as normal. In FY26, you are free to take off a total of 4 days at your convenience, to
celebrate and record as Family and Friends day.
Please follow the same guidelines as you would in Choosing and Recording Time Off.
How Support Handles Holidays
During Holidays, GitLab Support continues to operate as normal. Always book holidays as
required by your locality. For
non-statutory holidays, please follow the same guidelines as you would in Choosing and
Recording Time Off.
From late November through the first week of January, “the holiday season,”
each Support region takes additional steps to coordinate time off so that they
will maintain sufficient staffing levels to meet customer needs.
APAC
APAC plans for several high-impact holiday periods. Details of these dates are noted in the APAC
support page.
For end of year coverage, planning begins in September-October.
This typically involves determining the appropriate coverage based on both the volume of
incoming tickets during the holiday period the previous year, and the general ticket volume trend
since then.
The team then sign up in the planning issue to provide the necessary coverage by the end of
October. This allows time for discussion if anything is at risk.
Other factors taken into account for coverage include:
L&R expertise
CEOC, CMOC & Support-Manager on-call coverage - we prefer people covering on-call
roles to be working rather than doing it while they are on PTO, so we’ll generally seek to
swap shifts with those providing coverage
region coverage - as a wide region, we need to ensure we have sufficient people on in
both the first and second halves of the APAC day
shift coverage for 24/7 support
Previous planning issues can be seen in this epic.

These planning issues include an associated spreadsheet where Support Engineers can share
their holiday plans and coordinate coverage. This approach ensures:
Adequate coverage across all EMEA time zones during holiday periods
Visibility of team availability well in advance
Fair distribution of holiday time off
Sufficient expertise coverage (L&R, CEOC, CMOC, and on-call roles)
Team members are encouraged to add their plans to the spreadsheet as early as possible to
facilitate better coordination. While the team aims to accommodate all requested time off,
Support Engineers should avoid making firm commitments (such as purchasing nonrefundable
travel) until coverage has been confirmed by the management team.
AMER
AMER Support Managers will create and publish an issue in late September, using
this template.
In that issue they will invite everyone in AMER Support to use a specified Google
Form to indicate their preferences for days off during the holiday season. Once
the form has closed, around the 15th of October, the AMER Support managers will
quickly determine whether any changes need to be made to anybody’s requested
days off.
While it’s not very likely that anyone will be asked to work on a day that they
would like to take off, it is possible. For that reason, please don’t make any
firm commitments (such as purchasing a nonrefundable travel itinerary) until
the management team sends out notice that the schedule is confirmed.
Holiday time off ticket management
Guidance around ticket handling over global holiday time off is slightly different, because of the
potentially increased impact on the Support team.
The following peak holidays have the greatest potential to affect coverage:
Australia Day (26-January)
Republic Day (26-January)
Anzac Day (25-April)
International Workers Day (1-May)
Memorial Day (last Monday in May)
Independence Day (1-July to 4-July)
U.S. Thanksgiving Day (4th Thursday of November)
Immaculate Conception Day (8-Dec)
Christmas Day, St Stephens Day, Boxing Day, New Years Day and Day After New Years
(Period between 24-Dec to 2-Jan)
