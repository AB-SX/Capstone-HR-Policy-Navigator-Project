Shift Engineer Time Off
Planning Coverage for Time Off
Before their time off, Shift Engineers should find coverage for their weekend shifts. The following
people should be considered first for covering shifts:
Other Shift Engineers.
Support Engineers in the CEOC role leading up to the weekend.
Other Support Engineers, or ask a Manager for assistance finding coverage.
Coverage is only required for weekends days being taken off. All other days can follow the normal
time off recommendations.
Sick Time
If you are sick, page the on-call Support Manager as soon as you know you will be out on a
weekend.
Guidelines for Covering a Weekend Shift
If you are covering for a Shift Engineer, be mindful of what tickets are allowed to have public
replies posted during a weekend.
Holidays as a Shift Engineer
If a holiday occurs on a weekend, and you’d like to take it off, follow the guidance to plan
coverage for your time off.
You are also welcome to work the holiday, and float the holiday time to another day of your
choosing. It’s recommended that you float the holiday to any day within one month before or
after the holiday.
