Graduation from GitLab Academy
In order to be considered as having graduated from your CSMA onboarding journey, you need to
have completed the following:
Completed GitLab Academy eLearning prework on GitLab University
Attended all GitLab Academy sessions, including the Command of the Message class
(attendance is mandatory)
Attended all four role-based live skills sessions
Attended the in-person regional Academy Graduation Bootcamp and passed the stand-
and-deliver capstone assessment
Completed your role-based learning path on GitLab University
Completed the Pulse+ CSM Certificate I and Advanced CSM Certificate II
Attained the following certificates:
GitLab Certified Git Associate
GitLab Certified CI/CD Associate
GitLab Certified Project Management Associate
GitLab Certified Security Specialist
Completed role play activities and discussed feedback with your manager / onboarding
buddy
Shadowed at least 4 calls per week throughout your onboarding and discussed with
your manager

Buddy-led CSMA Onboarding Program
This program is an optional, self-paced supplement to the official GitLab onboarding experience
described above. It is not a requirement for graduation, but is designed to run alongside your
role-based learning path and GitLab Academy sessions — giving you and your onboarding buddy
a structured daily touchpoint while you are ramping into the role.
Overview
The CSMA Onboarding Program is a 4-week initiative built around daily focused learning
modules, practical assignments, and weekly mentor check-ins. Each module pairs a short video
with a hands-on assignment so that concepts are immediately applied to real CSMA workflows.
→ Start Here: Program Intro Video
The program lives in GitLab as a set of issues that you and your onboarding buddy work through
together. Each issue contains the learning content, relevant handbook links, and a practical
assignment for that day.
Program Details
Duration: 4 weeks, one module per business day
Participants: New hire CSMA + assigned onboarding buddy
Format: Daily video review → practical assignment → weekly mentor check-in
Effort: ~30 minutes per day alongside your main onboarding activities
How to Get Started
For new CSMAs
To get started, create your onboarding project (reach out to your buddy if you run into
problems!):
Go to the CS Training Programs subgroup (if you do not have access, ask your buddy or
manager for help)
Click Create Project
Click Create from template
Click Group
Next to CS Onboarding, click Use Template
Title the project [Your Name] Onboarding
Click Create project
Read the README.md and follow along!
Once you’ve set up your project, do the following each day until you have completed the
program:
Open the issue for today’s module and work through the content at your own pace.
Complete the assignment at the bottom of each issue — these are optional.
Bring your questions and assignment reflections to your weekly mentor check-in.
