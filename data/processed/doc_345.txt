For onboarding buddies
Review this Program Intro Video to get an understanding of the Onboarding Program
structure.
Ensure the new hire has correctly created their onboarding project and assist them if
needed.
Use the weekly issue structure to guide your check-in conversations — each week has a
natural theme to discuss.
The assignments are designed as conversation starters, not homework to review. Use
them to surface what’s clicking and where more context would help.
Make sure you share your own insights and experiences as you go through the
program; discuss your current customers and invite the new hire to your calls to shadow
so they can see how the work you’re doing together is applied in real-time.

Program Completion
After finishing Week 4, you’ll receive a final issue with reflection prompts to help you and your
mentor identify areas for continued development. You’ll also be invited to complete a short
feedback survey and schedule a debrief conversation.
After completing your onboarding, work with your onboarding buddy to get connected with the 
12-week CSMA Mentorship Program, a structured peer development initiative to pair CSMAs
who want to accelerate skill growth and share best practices with an experienced CSMA.
Onboarding Buddy — Reach out to @jgleason1 to discuss transition plan to Mentorship Program.
Expectations for Manager support during onboarding
As a new CSMA, your manager will be there to support you during your onboarding journey.
Beyond the expectations laid out for your manager in your onboarding issue, you can anticipate
they will also provide you support on the following:
Month 1
Schedule recurring 1:1s and check-in calls
Provide guidance on who the new team member should schedule coffee chats with
during their onboarding journey
Review and discuss company policies

Family and Friends first culture

Discuss any team-specific expectations
Review organizational structure
