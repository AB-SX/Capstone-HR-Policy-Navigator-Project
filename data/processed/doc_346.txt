The buddy should join the first 5-10 customer meetings led by the new CSMA. If the buddy is not
available, the manager should join instead.
It is not only for technical guidance but much more for general feedback and building
confidence in the CSMA role.
The close feedback loop will enable new CSMAs to learn faster by helping them
understand if their approach was the best one, where they can improve, and what went
well.
Generally, increased interaction between the onboarding buddy and new CSMA will
reduce the onboarding time.
Asking questions and feeling comfortable doing so, improved new hire satisfaction to
start working with clients.
