Feedback
Your feedback on the onboarding process is important to us and we welcome your thoughts and
ideas about how we can improve in the future. Please share your feedback in the following issue
for anything regarding the GitLab Academy: CSMA Onboarding Feedback. For feedback on the
buddy-led CSMA Onboarding Program, feel free to open an issue in the feedback project and tag
your onboarding buddy and manager.
Customer Handover
After you have completed both the general and the role-based onboarding and you feel
comfortable with working with customers, you will start the onboarding to your first customer
account. In case you are taking over your first customer from another CSMA, the Account
Handoff CSMA-to-CSMA Checklist is a very helpful resource. This handbook page also contains a 
list of questions that you can answer and afterwards discuss with the current CSMA on the
account. That way, the handover process will be smoother and you ensure that no important
customer topic is forgotten. When preparing for the account handover, take a look at the Google
Drive folder for the customer, Gainsight, Salesforce, Zendesk and the corresponding GitLab
collaboration project. In Gainsight, it is particularly helpful to go through the timeline and the
success plan for the customer to get a quick overview of ongoing topics, initiatives and issues.
CS Office Hours
We host weekly CS office hours meetings to help you answer questions in a judgment-free space.
You can add your questions to the agenda document linked in the Google Calendar invite and
hop on the call to work through questions or learn how to find helpful resources.
Helpful resources for faster onboarding
By talking to new hires and recent new CSMA team members, it was identified that the
onboarding buddy relationship plays a critical role for success of a new hire in the CSMA team.
Existing CSMAs explained that having a very close / short loop to their onboarding saw a close
relationship as a tremendous speed up of their onboarding.
Buddies should always be CSMAs (same department), to match the role as close as possible, as it
will help the new hire to resolve questions around their responsibilities and tasks quicker and
more efficient, compared to going through multiple chats or calls. To pick an onboarding buddy,
CSMA managers will use the guidance section for buddies from our general handbook section.
