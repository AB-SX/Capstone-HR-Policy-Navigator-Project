Offerings: GitLab Dedicated vs. GitLab SaaS vs. GitLab Self Managed
Self Managed: In GitLab Self-managed you will have full control over the server itself
and the environment
Dedicated: A single-tenant SaaS service for highly regulated and large enterprises
SaaS: The GitLab software-as-a-service offering. You don’t need to install anything to use
GitLab SaaS, you only need to sign up and start using GitLab straight away
