134. CSE Team Member Onboarding
CSE Team Member Onboarding
How new Customer Success Engineers ramp up: the learning modules and certifications, the live
sessions, the shadow and ramp framework, and the support new hires get from their manager
and buddy.
Onboarding as a Customer Success Engineer (CSE) is a guided, blended journey. It combines a
self-paced, role-based learning path, live virtual skills sessions, the
GitLab Revenue Academy with an in-person regional graduation event, and a practical
ramp from shadowing calls to leading them. This page explains what to expect and how your
manager and buddy support
you.
The formal learning and the practical ramp run in parallel, not one after the other. You are not
expected to finish
every module before your first shadow call, but you are expected to have enough depth at each
stage to be useful in
it.
Learning modules
Module 1: CSE at GitLab and call shadowing
Demonstrate understanding of GitLab basics and the DevSecOps lifecycle.
Express the Customer Success vision.
Describe the CSE role and how it fits in the wider Customer Experience organisation.
Set up a GitLab demo environment. You will use it in every technical call and lab during
your ramp.
Certification: GitLab Certified Git Associate.
Module 2: CSE deep dive
Describe a CSE’s rhythm of business.
Demonstrate a thorough understanding of CI/CD and apply it to customer scenarios. CI/
CD is the most common topic in
Pooled requests and central to onboarding, so complete this module before taking
requests on your own.
Complete a role-play exercise.
Certification: GitLab Certified CI/CD Associate.
