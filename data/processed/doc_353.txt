135. Solutions Architects Onboarding
Solutions Architects Onboarding
Onboarding Program for Solutions Architects (SA).
View the SA Handbook homepage for additional SA-related handbook pages.
Overview
The SA onboarding program is designed to get new Solutions Architects (SAs), Associate SAs
(ASAs), and SA Managers up to speed quickly and confidently. Onboarding combines self-paced
learning, live virtual sessions, and on-the-job support from your manager and SA buddy.
Questions? Join #sa-onboarding-hub in Slack.
Who This Program Is For
New Solutions Architects — full onboarding program
New Associate Solutions Architects (ASAs) — onboarding track mirrors the SA
program
New SA Managers — manager-specific onboarding track in development; see your
manager for interim guidance
Program Components
GitLab Academy
All new SAs participate in GitLab Academy — GitLab’s GTM onboarding program. GitLab Academy
includes a self-paced learning paths, virtual role-based workshops (SA Bootcamp), and a
mandatory in-person certification that culminates in a graduation ceremony.
SA-Specific Courses
New SAs complete two role-based courses in LevelUp covering the SA role, GitLab’s sales
methodology, how SAs work day to day, and the GitLab platform and key use cases.
SA Role-Based Certification
A certification completed as part of onboarding that validates readiness to engage with
customers independently.
For full details, see the GitLab Academy handbook page.
