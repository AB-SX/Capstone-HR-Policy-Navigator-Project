Supporting Resources
Your manager and the SA Enablement team will share onboarding guides, playbooks, and buddy
resources at the start of your onboarding. Reach out in #sa-onboarding-hub if you need access to
any of these materials.
Role Expectations
SA Manager
During onboarding, the SA manager is responsible for setting context, making introductions, and
ensuring the new SA has what they need to progress through the program. This includes
assigning an SA buddy, checking in regularly during the first 60 days, and being the primary
escalation point for any blockers.
SA Buddy
The SA buddy is a peer resource for the new SA during their first 30-60 days. The buddy helps the
new SA navigate day-to-day questions, understand how the team operates, and feel connected to
the broader SA organization. This is a coaching and support role, not a training role.
For detailed guidance on both roles, refer to the SA Onboarding Guide shared by the SA
Enablement team.
Getting Started
Your onboarding issue will walk you through the sequencing of all required activities. Your
manager and SA buddy are your primary points of contact for questions during your onboarding
period.

136. Graduating from Sales Onboarding
Graduating from Sales Onboarding
Graduation requirements, assessment criteria, and manager guidance for the GitLab Revenue
Academy.
The GitLab Revenue Academy graduation is designed to signal that a new hire is field-ready for
their role:
Able to pitch GitLab
Run value-based conversations
Connect our platform to measurable outcomes, not just complete passive learning.
Graduation is based on successful completion of all four Academy components plus a role-
specific stand-and-deliver assessment, with final decisions made at hiring manager discretion
within a consistent framework.
1. Core Graduation Requirements
New hires are expected to complete all four Academy components and meet the checkpoints
below. These requirements apply to all Academy cohorts unless otherwise noted.
Component 1: GitLab Foundations and Role-Based eLearning
New hires must:
Complete GitLab Foundations plus their role-based LevelUp/University learning
path (AE, SA, CSM, RM, xDR, etc.), including AI/Duo, DevOps, Security modernization
tracks, and Revenue Org intros.
Pass all associated knowledge checks in the assigned paths.
Reach the first checkpoint by approximately the end of Week 3 of onboarding.
Completion and scores are tracked in LevelUp/University and Academy progress reports and are
shared with hiring managers as a key input into graduation decisions.
Component 2: Value Sales Methodology (Command of the Message)
New hires must:
Attend the full Command of the Message (CoM) / value sales methodology class,
typically delivered as four 2-hour live virtual sessions covering GitLab’s sales
methodology, sales process, and value messaging.
Where live attendance is not possible (e.g., those in the APJ region), complete the full
set of recordings and any required practice sessions within the timelines
communicated for their cohort.
