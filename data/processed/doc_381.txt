Transition training module to LevelUp
How to transition a support training module to LevelUp
This document covers the process for transitioning existing GitLab Support training modules to 
LevelUp.

Transition process
Step 1 - Define transition goals
The first step in the transition is to visualize how the training will work on LevelUp, what new
components will be used, and which limitations you might have.

requesting and verifying an issue, such as an access request or pairing issue.
