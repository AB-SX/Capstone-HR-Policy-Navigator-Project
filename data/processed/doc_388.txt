153. Leadership Diversity Inclusion & Belonging
Council
Leadership Diversity Inclusion & Belonging Council
A strategic group of Senior leaders URG Leaders who assist the DIB team to implement the
strategy and align the strategy to the Division’s strategic imperatives
A strategic group of senior leaders who embed DIB strategy into business operations and provide
critical insights on divisional imperatives. Members bring diverse capabilities including
geographic expertise, division knowledge, and representation from underrepresented groups.
Responsibilities
Embed DIB strategic imperatives into business goals.
Unblock obstacles in global DIB strategy implementation.
Provide division and geographic insights to the DIB team.
Represent DIB in E-group and CEO Skips syncs.
Act as DIB Interruptors—actively advocate, challenge norms, and iterate to keep
Diversity, Inclusion and Belonging top of mind.
Structure & Members
Senior leadership representative (Sr. Dir+) from each department, plus a manager+ from an
underrepresented group where Sr. Dir+ URG representation isn’t available.
Engineering - Two Representatives
Product - Two Representatives
Sales - Two Representatives
Marketing - Two Representatives
Finance - One representative
Legal & Corporate Affairs - One representative
Security - One representative
People - Represented by DIB and the PBPs
People Business Partners
Office of CEO representative - One representative
