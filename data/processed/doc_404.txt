Sales Kickoff

Sales Kickoff (SKO) Overview
This event sets the tone for the GitLab field organization for the new fiscal year. The objective of
SKO is that team members will leave feeling…
Motivated: “Our product is helping our customers achieve amazing things and the vision for
our market is incredible. I know we have what it takes to win. I feel energized by the
opportunity to connect with team members in-person/virtually.”
Celebrated: “I feel like my team and leaders recognize and appreciate the work I do in
helping GitLab grow and succeed.”
Enabled: “I am confident that I have the skills and resources needed to excel in my territory/
role and predictably Land with vision and Expand with purpose. I feel supported by Marketing
and Product teams who want to help me win.”
We will achieve this by creating an event that is:
Immersive
Engaging
Inclusive
