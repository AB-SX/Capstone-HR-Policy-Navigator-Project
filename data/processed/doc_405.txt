Preparing for your meeting
In order to have the best meeting, preparation prior to the meeting is key. The following action
items are ways to prep for a customer meeting:
Look over notes of previous engagement and keep handy for customer meeting. Write key items
to remember during the meeting - make sure to cover these items throughout the meeting.
Look over your agenda and notes for call.
Do research on the company and person(s) and get an understanding of the current state of
affairs at their company.
Look over the basic principles of customer meetings and resources in GitLab handbook and make
sure you’re ready for the meeting.
