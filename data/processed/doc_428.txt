Is there opportunity to participate in the program if I am an aspiring manager?
We offer the ElevatePrep program, specifically designed for GitLab individual contributors (ICs)
who would like to one day step into a people management role or lead multifaceted, cross-
functional projects more effectively. ElevatePrep lays the foundation for strong professional skills
that support a variety of goals.
Please reach out to the Talent and Development team in the #learninganddevelopment if you
wish to be considered for the next round. We’ll be sharing more details on the program soon.
What if I get promoted while I’m part of Elevate?
If you’re promoted from Manager to Senior Manager, you’ll continue Elevate without interruption.
If you’re promoted to a Director role or above, please plan to discuss directly with your manager
and the Elevate team. Decisions will be made on a case by case basis.

169. Elevate Applied
Elevate Applied
Elevate Applied: Continuous Leadership Development Beyond Certification
Elevate Applied is a comprehensive post-certification program designed to support leaders who
have completed ElevatePrep, Elevate or Elevate+. This program provides ongoing resources,
coaching, and learning opportunities to help you continuously develop your leadership skills and
apply proven methodologies in your daily work.
Please note: Elevate Applied is currently under development. Content and offerings will be expanded
and refined based on participant feedback and program evolution.
Continuous Learning Philosophy
Elevate Applied operates on the principle that leadership development is an ongoing journey, not
a destination. The program provides multiple pathways for continued growth:
Apply - Put Elevate concepts into practice with real workplace challenges
Connect - Build relationships with other leaders facing similar challenges
Develop - Continuously refine and expand your leadership capabilities
Contribute - Share your learnings and mentor others in their leadership journey
Program Benefits
Hone Membership License
Premium Access to Elevate-Style Learning Experiences
Complimentary Hone membership for all GitLab team members
Access to live, interactive learning sessions with industry experts
On-demand leadership development content library
Peer learning opportunities with leaders from other organizations
Skill-building workshops aligned with Elevate methodologies
Flexible scheduling to accommodate your work commitments
