172. The GitLab Mentorship Program hosted by the
Women TMRG
The GitLab Mentorship Program hosted by the
Women TMRG
Mentorship opportunities for those that identify as women at GitLab first and is open to all
pending mentor participation. The program is sponsored by a partnership between the Women
TMRG, other TMxGs and the GitLab DIB team
The GitLab Mentorship program hosted by the Women TMRG is a 5-month career development
opportunity for participants to build trust across the organization, enable pathways for internal
growth and development, and increase collaboration across teams.
Questions, comments, and feedback can be posted in the #women-at-gitlab-mentorship slack
channel.

History
In 2020, the Women’s TMRG partnered with the Sales Organization at GitLab to offer the Women
in Sales Mentorship Program pilot. This initial iteration was a huge success and inspired the scale
of this iteration to include a wider audience. The program now runs on a yearly basis and has
been expanded to include mentees who identify as women and non-binary team members.
Please see the results section for a list of sessions since launch.
