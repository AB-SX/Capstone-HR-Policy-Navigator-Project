Purpose of the TMRG
The GitLab’s Gente TMRG provides a safe space for every GitLab Team member within GitLab to
raise awareness and celebrate Latin American culture, and to understand how the diversity of this
region can be leveraged to contribute to GitLab’s strategic objectives. This group is not
exclusionary and is open to anyone who is a GitLab employee and interested in learning more
about Latin culture.
Mission
To promote advancement, cultural awareness, talent recruitment, retention, and professional
development for Latinx individuals by giving a voice to the diverse backgrounds of the Latinx
community in order to ensure the GitLab messaging continues to reflect its core values of
Diversity, Inclusion & Belonging.
