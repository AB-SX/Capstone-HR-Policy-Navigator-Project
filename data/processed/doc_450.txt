181. TMRG - GitLab Pride
TMRG - GitLab Pride
We are the GitLab Pride Team Member Resource Group (TMRG) founded in the fall of 2019. Learn
more!
This group strives to connect employees at GitLab that are part of the LGBTQ+ community, or are
allies, with professional and personal opportunities to meet others, speak at events, and share
their lived experiences to improve and strengthen our community.
Mission
To ensure that GitLab is proactive in supporting, retaining, and recruiting from the LGBTQ+ and
gender minority community. This group also aims to help develop and coordinate mentoring in
the workplace.
This group strives to connect employees at GitLab that are part of the LGBTQ+ or gender minority
community, or are allies, with professional and personal opportunities to meet others, speak at
events, and share their lived experiences to improve and strengthen our community. As gender
minorities is a broad term, this includes, but is not limited to: Those that are transgender,
nonbinary, agender, genderqueer, bigender, neutrois, gender questioning, genderfluid, and more.
