TMRG - GitLab Women
An overview of our remote TMRG GitLab Women
Mission
The GitLab Women TMRG cultivates an inclusive community where those who identify as women
can build skills, leadership and connection through mentorship, collaboration and professional
development. This group is open to all members of the GitLab community.
Vision
To build an interconnected, barrier-free GitLab community where everyone who identifies as a
woman is professionally equipped to drive innovation, socially supported to thrive, and globally
empowered to shape the future of technology.

How to Join
Please sign up here to be added to the Women TMRG Google Group. This will ensure
you’re added to upcoming TMRG calendar events. It might take up to 24 hours before
the calendar events appear on your calendar. If there is an event happening within
24 hours of you joining the Google Group, please post in the #women Slack channel
to be added manually.

Member Involvement
This section is meant to outline how team members in the Women TMRG can contribute to key
initiatives each quarter. We encourage team members to contribute in any way they feel
comfortable and have capacity - from meeting attendance to leading initiatives and anywhere in
between!
Attend the Monthly Women TMRG Meeting
We host one Women TMRG meeting each month, offered in three sessions to accommodate
members across regions. The meeting brings together planning and social connection, with the
format shaped by the activities and priorities for that month. Check the calendar invitation for the
current date, time, and meeting details, as these may vary from month to month.
A typical meeting may include:
Quick updates from TMRG leads on monthly and quarterly priorities. Updates may be
shared and discussed synchronously or asynchronously.
Discussion of projects and ideas related to our TMRG pillars: Awareness & Education,
Professional Development, Recruiting & Retention, and Business Influence.
Time to connect socially, which may include small breakout groups and open
conversation, depending on the group and available time.
Some months, a special event, such as a guest speaker session or workshop, may take the place
of the usual meeting format. Check the #women Slack channel for that month’s agenda and
format.
Allies are always encouraged to attend any TMRG meeting. For questions or to share ideas, reach
out in the #women Slack channel.
