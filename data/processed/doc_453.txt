Use the TMRG Issue Board to Create or Contribute to a TMRG key initiative
We use our GitLab issue board to coordinate and prioritize projects on a quarterly basis.
Contribute to the issue board by:
Open a new issue to propose an idea or project using the key-initiative-proposal
issue template (please note that you don’t have to be available to lead the project -
opening a topic for discussion is great!)
Apply labels as necessary to track the TMRG focus areas. The labels are: Awareness &
Education, Diversity - Inclusion and belonging, Recruiting & Retention, and 
Outreach & Business Development
Contribute to an open issue that is assigned to a quarterly milestone. These milestones
outline the current focus projects of the TMRG
Attend or notify others of upcoming events using the Events - Womens TMRG label and
consider using the event-or-conference-fyi issue template. Assign event issues to the
relevant milestone based on event date.
Participate in a Candidate TMRG Call
The hiring process at GitLab includes an optional step where candidates can request to meet with
a TMRG team member. As a TMRG member, you can volunteer to speak with candidates to
answer questions and share your experience at GitLab.
Join the #candidate_tmrg-call Slack channel to participate.
