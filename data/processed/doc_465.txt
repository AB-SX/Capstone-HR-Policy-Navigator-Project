Inclusive Practices

Goal
The DIB Inclusive Practices Working Group aims to improve the use of Inclusive Practices in
meetings at GitLab and with external parties.
Overview
Inclusive Practices are essential for creating a workplace where everyone feels valued and can
bring a diverse set of skills and qualities to the organization. The focus of the DIB Working Group
is to create inclusive practices to be leveraged in team meetings through name pronunciations
and time zones.
Goals
This is a non-comprehensive list of topics to be discussed.
What is the primary goal of Inclusive Practices in Meetings
What is the metric for success both long and short term
Establish the most important needs for Inclusive Meetings
How to develop guidance on name pronunciation
Guidance on do’s and don’t with for inclusive meetings
Not an exhaustive list
