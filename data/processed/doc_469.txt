NeuroInclusion in Practice
Design for every way of thinking
At GitLab, neuroinclusion is part of how we live our CREDIT values.
When we design our systems and communication for every way of thinking, everyone benefits —
collaboration improves, innovation strengthens, and all team members can thrive.
This page introduces the NeuroInclusion in Practice learning series and connects you to related
resources, community spaces, and support.
About NeuroInclusion in Practice
Purpose
NeuroInclusion in Practice helps build confidence, shared language, and practical skills so every
GitLab team member can work effectively in our async-first, global environment.
The programme combines lived experience, research, and practical guidance for everyday
collaboration and leadership.

Key Principles
These principles align with GitLab’s CREDIT values — especially Collaboration, Diversity, Inclusion
& Belonging, and Results.
Design for every brain — everyone benefits
Designing systems and processes that consider different ways of thinking supports
collaboration and innovation across all teams.
Barriers live in systems, not people
Inclusion focuses on improving environments and processes rather than expecting
individuals to adapt to systems that don’t work for them.
Clarity and flexibility enable success
Clear expectations combined with adaptable approaches help people work effectively in
a global, async environment.
Different thinking styles are strengths
Cognitive diversity drives creative problem-solving, innovation, and better outcomes for
both teams and customers.
