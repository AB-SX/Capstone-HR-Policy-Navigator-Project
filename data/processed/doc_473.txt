193. Sales Kickoff 2022
Sales Kickoff 2022
GitLab Sales Kickoff 2022
Overview
GitLab SKO 2022 took place virtually from March 15-17, 2022. The virtual event consisted of three
half-days offered in two time zone options to accommodate our team members around the
world. To learn more about the event, see the Field Team FAQ document.
