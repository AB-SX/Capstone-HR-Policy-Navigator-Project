GitLab Sales Kickoff 2021
Overview
GitLab Sales Kickoff 2021 consisted of half-day virtual sessions (due to COVID-19) during the week
of Feb 8-11, 2021. Sessions were repeated twice to accommodate our team members around the
world, and the 2021 theme was Peak Performance.
