SKO “Call for Content”
In order to drive relentless prioritization based on GitLab Sales Kickoff (SKO) theme/sub-themes/
goals, gather content in a timely manner and secure executive consensus early, the SKO Core
Team implements a “Call for Content” for SKO breakout sessions. A secondary benefit of this
process is that it gives Field Enablement a strong backlog of well-rounded training and
enablement topics for topic submissions not selected or prioritized for SKO (e.g. webcast topics,
etc.) in future months/quarters to support continuous education initiatives for the field
organization. There are two types of breakout sessions:
Role-Based Breakout Sessions: Teaching skills and behaviors aligned to specific role(s)
within the organization.
Submit a role-based breakout session idea using this issue template
“Choose Your Own Adventure” Breakout Sessions: Allow attendees to step out of the
role-based breakout experience and select from a list of breakout topics for the final
breakout. These topics can be a deep-dive into a keynote topic (ex. diving into a feature
that Product discussed in their keynote) or a broader topic or soft-skill (ex. DIB in Sales,
Project Management, etc.).
Request a topic or focus for a “Choose Your Own Adventure” breakout using this issue
template
Note that we will also use the Call for Content submission form for Mainstage sessions to tease
out the learning objectives and outlines, but we will not implement a formal selection process as
there is already a prescriptive “formula” for which speakers/teams give keynotes.
