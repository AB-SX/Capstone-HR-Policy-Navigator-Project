204. Templates & Tools
Templates & Tools
Index of key TMxG leadership templates and tools, with guidance on when to use each.
This page lists the core templates and tools available to TMxG leaders and explains when to
use each one.
Actual Google Doc/Sheet templates live in an internal Drive folder. For direct links and ownership,
see the internal Templates Index in the TMxG Leaders Resource Hub.
How to use these templates
For each template:
Open the link (view‑only).
Go to File → Make a copy.
Save your copy in your TMxG’s shared Drive folder.
Share with your DIB partner and co‑leads as needed.
Planning templates
Quarterly Planning Template
Use for: Documenting your plan for each fiscal quarter (monthly activities, quarterly
initiative, budget needs, key dates and DRIs).
When: At the start of each quarter, or when re‑planning mid‑quarter.
Annual Overview Template
Use for: Mapping cultural observance months, major initiatives, and partnership
opportunities across the fiscal year.
When: During annual planning (typically late Q4 for the next year).
Budget Request Template
Use for: Requesting budget for events or programs (speaker fees, partnerships, swag,
accessibility adjustments).
When: At least 4 weeks before you need funds.
Budget Tracking Spreadsheet
Use for: Tracking approved budget, spend, and remaining balance across the year.

Event templates
Event Planning Checklist
Use for: Step‑by‑step planning for a quarterly‑type event from 4 weeks out through
follow‑up.
When: Anytime you’re planning a speaker session, panel, workshop, or large
observance event.
Event Announcement Template (Slack/Loop)
Use for: Quickly drafting promotional messages for Slack and Loop.
When: 2–3 weeks before your event, and again 1 week and 1 day before.
Cross‑TMxG Collaboration Proposal
Use for: Proposing a joint event or initiative with another TMxG, capturing shared goals
and responsibilities.
When: Before committing to cross‑group events that will require shared planning and
budget.
Meeting templates
Executive Sponsor Meeting Agenda
Use for: Structured conversations with your Executive Sponsor (initial engagement and
quarterly check‑ins).
When:
At the start of a new sponsorship relationship.
At least once per quarter.
TMxG Leadership Team Meeting Agenda
Use for: Regular syncs with co‑leads or an expanded leadership “board”.
When: Monthly or as needed when coordinating larger initiatives.
Tracking & reporting templates
Shared Activity Tracker (Google Sheet)
Use for: Logging all TMxG activities (monthly engagement, quarterly initiatives, allyship
events) and attendance.
